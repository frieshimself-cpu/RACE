### Attempt: Obstruction to a Direct Positivity Proof of RH via the Completed Xi-Function

Let \(\xi(s) = \frac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)\). The functional equation \(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\) and the Hadamard product
\[
\xi(s)=\xi(0)\prod_{\rho}\Bigl(1-\frac{s}{\rho}\Bigr)
\]
(product over the non-trivial zeros, taken in conjugate pairs and with the usual convergence factor) are classical. Taking the logarithmic derivative yields the identity
\[
\frac{\xi'}{\xi}(s)=\sum_{\rho}\frac1{s-\rho}.
\]
Evaluating on the critical line \(s=\frac12+it\) (\(t\in\mathbb{R}\)) and taking real parts produces
\[
\operatorname{Re}\frac{\xi'}{\xi}\Bigl(\tfrac12+it\Bigr)
=\sum_{\rho}\frac{\sigma_{\rho}-\frac12}{(\sigma_{\rho}-\frac12)^2+(t-t_{\rho})^2},
\]
where \(\rho=\sigma_{\rho}+it_{\rho}\). The right-hand side is non-negative for all \(t\) if and only if every \(\sigma_{\rho}=\frac12\). Consequently RH is equivalent to the pointwise inequality
\[
\operatorname{Re}\frac{\xi'}{\xi}\Bigl(\tfrac12+it\Bigr)\ge0\qquad\forall t\in\mathbb{R}.
\]

A natural attempt to prove the inequality is to insert the Dirichlet series for \(-\zeta'/\zeta\) (valid for \(\operatorname{Re}s>1\)) into the expression for \(\xi'/\xi\), move the contour leftward, and exploit the rapid decay of the Gamma factor together with the known zero-free region \(\sigma\ge1-c/\log(|t|+2)\). After collecting the contribution of the trivial zeros and the polar terms at \(s=0,1\), one obtains an explicit formula whose continuous main term is essentially
\[
\tfrac12\log\frac{t}{2\pi}-\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}\frac{\Gamma'}{\Gamma}\Bigl(\tfrac14+i\tfrac t2\Bigr)
\]
plus an absolutely convergent sum over primes of the form
\[
\sum_{n=2}^\infty\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,n^{-it}\,W\Bigl(\log n,t\Bigr),
\]
where the weight \(W\) arises from the horizontal integrals and is positive and rapidly decreasing in \(\log n\).

If the oscillatory sum could be shown to be dominated by the main term for every \(t\), the desired positivity would follow. The obstruction is quantitative: the best available bounds on the prime-power sum (even under RH itself) are of size \(\asymp\sqrt t\,(\log t)^C\) on vertical segments of length 1, while the main term grows only like \(\log t\). The error therefore exceeds the main term infinitely often unless a massive cancellation, stronger than any known pair-correlation estimate, is assumed. In particular, the Montgomery pair-correlation conjecture supplies square-root cancellation on average but does not control the pointwise supremum needed here. Thus the contour-shift argument cannot close, and the positivity of \(\operatorname{Re}(\xi'/\xi)\) on the critical line remains inaccessible by this method.

(The same obstruction appears, mutatis mutandis, when one tries to verify Li’s criterion \(\lambda_n\ge0\) by expressing the \(\lambda_n\) as moments of the same explicit formula.)

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Contour-shift positivity for Re(ξ'/ξ) blocked by insufficient pointwise prime-sum cancellation
