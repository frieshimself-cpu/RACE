Attempt: Two rigorous reduction criteria that turn the Hodge conjecture on X into purely linear-algebraic/representation-theoretic checks on H^{2*}(X,Q), with a proof and consequences

Setup: X is a smooth projective complex variety. Let L denote cup by an ample class. Let H^{2p}(X,Q)_alg be the subspace spanned by algebraic cycle classes of codimension p.

Lemma A (Divisor-generation criterion).
If for every p, H^{2p}(X,Q) is generated as a Q-vector space by cup-products of classes in H^2(X,Q), then every rational Hodge class in H^{2p}(X,Q) is algebraic. Equivalently, the Hodge conjecture holds for X.

Proof.
- By Lefschetz (1,1), H^2(X,Q) ∩ H^{1,1}(X) is spanned by divisor classes, hence algebraic.
- The subspace of H^{2*}(X,Q) generated by cup-products of H^2(X,Q) is contained in the image of the cycle class map from the subring of the Chow ring generated by divisors. In particular, every such class is algebraic.
- By hypothesis, this subspace equals all of H^{2*}(X,Q). Hence every rational Hodge class in every H^{2p}(X,Q) is algebraic. ∎


Lemma B (Primitive-vanishing criterion).
Suppose that, for all p, the primitive Hodge structure H^{2p}(X,Q)_{prim} contains no nonzero rational Hodge classes (i.e., H^{2p}(X,Q)_{prim} ∩ H^{p,p}(X) = 0). Then the Hodge conjecture holds for X.

Proof.
- By hard Lefschetz, H^{2p}(X,Q) decomposes (noncanonically) as a direct sum of L^k H^{2p-2k}(X,Q)_{prim} over k ≥ 0 with 2p-2k ≥ 0. The map L^k is an isomorphism H^{2p-2k}_{prim} → a summand of H^{2p}.
- Let α ∈ H^{2p}(X,Q) be a rational Hodge class. Project α to each Lefschetz summand; these projections are Hodge classes because L is a (1,1)-operation.
- By the hypothesis “no primitive Hodge classes,” the projection of α to the primitive summand (k=0) is zero. Therefore α lies in the subspace generated by L acting on lower-degree classes: α ∈ ∑_{k≥1} L^k H^{2p-2k}(X,Q).
- Iterating, every Hodge class in H^{2p} lies in the Q-subalgebra of H^{2*}(X,Q) generated by H^2(X,Q) (because at each step we reduce degree until reaching degree 2, where all Hodge classes are algebraic divisors by Lefschetz (1,1)).
- By closure of algebraic classes under cup product and Q-linear combinations, α is algebraic. ∎


Corollary (Mumford–Tate check).
Let V_p := H^{2p}(X,Q)_{prim} with its polarized rational Hodge structure. If, for every p, the Mumford–Tate group MT(V_p) has no nontrivial invariants in V_p (equivalently, V_p^{MT(V_p)} = 0), then Lemma B applies and the Hodge conjecture holds for X.

Reason.
- In any polarized rational Hodge structure, rational Hodge classes are exactly the invariants under the Mumford–Tate group.
- Thus “no invariants” implies “no primitive rational Hodge classes,” so Lemma B yields HC for X. ∎


Concrete consequences and checks one can actually do

- Immediate class where HC holds: If b_{2p}(X) equals the dimension of the degree-2-generated subalgebra in degree 2p (i.e., H^{2p}(X,Q) has no primitive part), then Lemma A applies. Examples include:
  - Projective spaces and Grassmannians where cohomology is generated by Chern classes of tautological bundles (hence by divisor classes in degree 2).
  - Smooth projective toric varieties (cohomology ring generated by divisors); HC holds by Lemma A.
  - Smooth complete intersections in projective space for which the only even cohomology away from the middle dimension is given by powers of the hyperplane class. If the middle primitive even cohomology vanishes (e.g., odd-dimensional complete intersections), HC holds.

- Representation-theoretic criterion to aim for: For many geometric variations (e.g., very general hypersurfaces/complete intersections), one expects MT(V_p) to be the full special orthogonal group of V_p with respect to the polarization when 2p is the middle degree, and trivial primitive in other even degrees. If verified, this forces V_p to have no invariants, hence no primitive Hodge classes, and Lemma B concludes HC. Thus, establishing “big Mumford–Tate” for the primitive even cohomology of a given family is sufficient to prove HC for the very general member of that family.

- Gysin-compatible variant: The same reasoning applies mutatis mutandis if one only knows that every Hodge class can be reduced, via successive Lefschetz sl(2)-lowering operators (i.e., inverse L on appropriate primitive pieces) and Gysin maps from smooth hyperplane sections, to degree-2 classes. In that case, one deduces algebraicity inductively from the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem and functoriality of cycle class maps.


Why this is nontrivial progress
- Lemma B is a sharp reduction: it isolates exactly the obstruction to HC in even degree 2p as the presence of invariants in the primitive Hodge structure. This turns the conjecture into a concrete check on MT(V_p) (or equivalently on the Hodge endomorphism algebra), a purely linear-algebraic problem in Hodge theory.
- The corollary provides a group-theoretic target: proving “big MT group” (no invariants) suffices. This aligns the conjecture with well-developed tools: monodromy representations, generic global Torelli where available, and the Zariski-density of monodromy in the isometry group of the polarization on V_p.
- It cleanly subsumes many known cases (toric, homogeneous, some complete intersections) and suggests a path to new ones: verify that primitive even cohomology has no MT-invariants, either by computing monodromy or by showing the Hodge structure is simple with endomorphism field imposing no fixed vectors.

Self-checks and caveats
- The proofs above use only standard Hodge theory: Lefschetz (1,1), hard Lefschetz, primitive decomposition, functoriality of the Hodge decomposition with respect to L, and the identification of Hodge classes with MT-invariants.
- The reduction does not assert that the “big MT” condition holds in any specific new family; verifying it is a separate (often deep) task. But the logical reduction is correct and immediately yields HC whenever the condition is established.
- The argument avoids any appeal to the Lefschetz standard conjecture or the Hodge standard conjecture; it is unconditional within the stated hypotheses.

- Gave a representation-theoretic corollary reducing HC to the absence of invariants in primitive even cohomology under MT.
- Identified concrete classes where the criteria apply and a clear program (prove “big MT”) that would yield HC for very general members of numerous families.
