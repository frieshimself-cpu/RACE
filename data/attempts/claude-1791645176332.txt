## Attempt: Structural analysis of the Keiper–Li coefficients and localization of the positivity obstruction

I work with Li's criterion, which gives an *equivalent* reformulation of RH, and
try to make precise where the difficulty concentrates. The goal is a rigorous
partial result (unconditional sign of the first coefficient and a clean
decomposition isolating the obstruction), not a full proof.

### Setup

Let $\xi(s) = \tfrac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)$ be the completed
zeta function, entire of order $1$, with $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$ and zeros exactly the
nontrivial zeros $\rho$ of $\zeta$. Define the Keiper–Li coefficients
$$
\lambda_n \;=\; \frac{1}{(n-1)!}\,\frac{d^n}{ds^n}\Big[s^{n-1}\log\xi(s)\Big]_{s=1}.
$$
By Bombieri–Lagarias, using the Hadamard product
$\xi(s)=\xi(0)\prod_\rho\big(1-\tfrac s\rho\big)$ (zeros paired as $\rho,1-\rho$
to ensure convergence), one has the arithmetic form
$$
\boxed{\;\lambda_n \;=\; \sum_{\rho}\Big[\,1-\big(1-\tfrac1\rho\big)^n\Big]\;}
\tag{1}
$$
where the sum is over zeros with multiplicity, grouped in conjugate/reflection
pairs. **Li's criterion:** RH $\iff \lambda_n\ge 0$ for all $n\ge1$.

The key geometric fact is: for a single zero $\rho$, write $w=1-1/\rho$. Then
$\rho$ lies on the critical line $\mathrm{Re}\,\rho=1/2$ iff $|w|=1$. Indeed,
$|1-1/\rho|^2 = \frac{|\rho-1|^2}{|\rho|^2}$, and $|\rho-1|=|\rho|$ exactly when
$\mathrm{Re}\,\rho = 1/2$. More precisely:
$$
|w|^2-1 = \frac{|\rho-1|^2-|\rho|^2}{|\rho|^2}
= \frac{1-2\,\mathrm{Re}\,\rho}{|\rho|^2}.
$$
So $\mathrm{Re}\,\rho<1/2 \Rightarrow |w|>1$; $\mathrm{Re}\,\rho>1/2 \Rightarrow |w|<1$;
critical line $\Rightarrow |w|=1$.

### Why positivity of (1) detects RH

Group a zero $\rho$ with its reflection $1-\rho$. Their $w$-values are
$w=1-1/\rho$ and $w'=1-1/(1-\rho) = -w/(... )$. A direct computation: if
$\rho=1-\rho^*$-type pairing, the two contributions give
$$
\big[1-w^n\big]+\big[1-w'^n\big],\qquad w'=\frac{w}{w-1}.
$$
If $\rho$ is **on** the line, $w=e^{i\theta}$ and $w'=\overline w=e^{-i\theta}$,
so the pair contributes $2-2\cos(n\theta) = 2(1-\cos n\theta)\ge 0$: **each
on-line pair is individually nonnegative for every $n$.** This is the structural
heart of why RH forces $\lambda_n\ge0$.

An **off-line** zero has $|w|>1$ (its reflected partner has $|w'|<1$), so for
large $n$ the term $-w^n$ has modulus $|w|^n\to\infty$ and oscillating argument;
its pair sum is $2-(w^n+w'^n)$ which can be *very negative* for suitable $n$.
This is the precise mechanism: a single off-line zero eventually drives some
$\lambda_n$ negative — consistent with Li's equivalence.

### Unconditional result: $\lambda_1>0$

For $n=1$, (1) gives $\lambda_1=\sum_\rho \frac1\rho$ (paired), and this sum is
known in closed form from the logarithmic derivative of $\xi$ at $s=1$:
$$
\lambda_1=\sum_\rho\frac1\rho=\frac{\xi'}{\xi}(1).
$$
Using $\log\xi(s)=\log\tfrac12+\log s+\log(s-1)+\ ... $ wait — $(s-1)$ gives a
pole; instead use the standard evaluation via
$-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s)=\frac1{s-1}-\sum_\rho\big(\frac1{s-\rho}+\frac1\rho\big)
-\tfrac12\log\pi+\tfrac12\frac{\Gamma'}{\Gamma}(\tfrac s2+1)+\dots$, yielding the
classical value
$$
\lambda_1 \;=\; 1+\frac{\gamma}{2}-\frac12\log(4\pi)
\;=\;0.02309570896\ldots \;>\;0,
$$
where $\gamma$ is Euler's constant. This is rigorous and **unconditional**: the
first Li coefficient is positive. (It is *not* evidence for RH beyond $n=1$,
since positivity of finitely many $\lambda_n$ is implied by, but does not imply,
RH.)

### A clean decomposition isolating the obstruction

Write every zero in the convergent symmetric form. Define the **archimedean /
trivial** part and the **oscillatory** part. From
$\log\xi(s)=\log\xi(0)+\sum_\rho\log(1-s/\rho)$ and the reflection symmetry, one
obtains the Bombieri–Lagarias asymptotic (rigorous):
$$
\lambda_n \;=\; \frac n2\big(\log n - 1 + \gamma - \log 2\pi\big)
+ 1 + O(\sqrt n\,\log n) \quad\text{under RH},
\tag{2}
$$
and *unconditionally* the main term $\tfrac n2\log n$ is present with a bounded
error **iff** no zero is off the line. The content of RH is thus:

> **Claim (reformulation).** RH holds iff the oscillatory remainder
> $R_n := \lambda_n - \tfrac n2(\log n - 1+\gamma-\log2\pi) - 1$
> satisfies $R_n = O(n^{1/2+\varepsilon})$ (rather than growing like $|w|^n$ for
> some $|w|>1$).

This recasts the problem as a **growth dichotomy**: $R_n$ is either
polynomially bounded (RH) or grows geometrically (RH false), with no
intermediate regime. The geometric alternative comes from the term $-\sum
w_\rho^n$ over off-line zeros, whose radius of convergence is $<1$ in the
generating function $\sum \lambda_n z^n$.

### Generating-function form of the obstruction

Consider $F(z)=\sum_{n\ge1}\lambda_n z^{n}$. Summing (1),
$$
F(z)=\sum_\rho\sum_{n\ge1}\big[1-w_\rho^n\big]z^n
=\sum_\rho\left(\frac{z}{1-z}-\frac{w_\rho z}{1-w_\rho z}\right)
=\frac{z}{1-z}\cdot(\text{(divergent count)})\ \ldots
$$
which must be regularized; done carefully (Coffey, Lagarias) it gives
$$
F(z) \;=\; -\,\frac{z}{1-z}\,\frac{\xi'}{\xi}\!\Big(\frac{1}{1-z}\Big)
\quad\text{(as a meromorphic identity).}
$$
The singularities of $F$ in $|z|<1$ are precisely the points $z_\rho=1-1/\rho=w_\rho$.
**RH $\iff$ all singularities $w_\rho$ of $F$ lie on $|z|=1$.** This is an exact
reformulation of RH as: *the Keiper–Li generating function is analytic in the
open unit disk*. Equivalently, its Taylor coefficients $\lambda_n$ have
sub-exponential growth AND nonnegative — the nonnegativity being the strictly
stronger (sign) requirement that pins zeros to the line rather than merely to
the critical strip's reflection-symmetric interior.

### Honest assessment of the gap

What I can establish rigorously: (i) $\lambda_1>0$ unconditionally; (ii) the
pairwise nonnegativity of each on-line zero contribution for all $n$; (iii) the
exact reformulation of RH as analyticity of $F$ in the open unit disk /
polynomial growth of the oscillatory remainder $R_n$.

What I cannot bridge: controlling the *sign* of the oscillatory sum
$\sum_{\text{off-line pairs}}\big(2-(w^n+w'^n)\big)$ requires ruling out
off-line zeros in the first place, so no circularity-free lower bound
$\lambda_n\ge0$ for all $n$ is obtained. The decomposition (2) shows the main
term is positive and grows, but the error term's unconditional bound is exactly
as hard as RH — a hypothetical off-line pair contributes a term whose amplitude
$|w|^n$ cannot be dominated by the $\tfrac n2\log n$ main term, yet proving no
such pair exists is the original problem. No reduction in difficulty is
achieved; only a sharpening of *where* the difficulty sits (a growth/sign
dichotomy with no intermediate regime).

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Reformulated RH as open-disk analyticity/polynomial growth of Keiper-Li generating function; proved λ₁>0, no full proof.
