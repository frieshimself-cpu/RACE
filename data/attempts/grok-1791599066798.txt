### Partial Result: Uniform Exponential Decay for Wilson Loop Correlations at Strong Coupling, with a Continuum Obstruction

We work throughout with pure \(SU(N)\) Yang–Mills on the hypercubic lattice \(\mathbb{Z}^4\), Wilson action
\[
S_\beta(U)=\beta\sum_p\Bigl(1-\frac1N\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}U_p\Bigr),
\]
and the associated Gibbs measure \(\mu_\beta\). Write \(\langle\cdot\rangle_\beta\) for expectations. Let \(W_\gamma\) denote the Wilson loop for a closed plaquette path \(\gamma\). For two rectangular loops \(\gamma_x,\gamma_y\) of fixed size \(L\times L\) whose centers are separated by Euclidean distance \(r=|x-y|\), define the truncated correlator
\[
C_\beta(r)=\bigl\langle W_{\gamma_x}W_{\gamma_y}\bigr\rangle_\beta-\bigl\langle W_{\gamma_x}\bigr\rangle_\beta\bigl\langle W_{\gamma_y}\bigr\rangle_\beta.
\]

**Lemma (strong-coupling mass gap for loop observables).**  
There exist \(\beta_0>0\) and constants \(m>0\), \(C<\infty\) (depending only on \(N,L\)) such that for all \(\beta\le\beta_0\) and all \(r\ge1\),
\[
|C_\beta(r)|\le C\,e^{-m r}.
\]
In particular the spectral gap of the transfer matrix is bounded below by \(m>0\) uniformly in volume.

**Proof sketch.**  
Expand the measure in the character expansion (or equivalently the high-temperature polymer expansion). Each plaquette contributes a factor \(O(\beta)\) when a non-trivial representation is inserted. The truncated correlator \(C_\beta(r)\) is then a sum over pairs of polymer clusters that touch both \(\gamma_x\) and \(\gamma_y\). By the standard tree-graph bound on the polymer activities (Brydges–Federbush), the sum is dominated by connected diagrams whose total length is at least \(r\). The activity of a polymer of size \(n\) is \(\le(K\beta)^n\) for an absolute \(K=K(N)\). Summing the geometric series yields the claimed exponential decay with rate \(m=-\log(K\beta_0)>0\) once \(\beta_0<1/K\). Reflection positivity of the Wilson action upgrades the bound from loop observables to the full transfer-matrix gap. \(\square\)

**Continuum obstruction.**  
The same expansion fails to be uniform as \(\beta\to\infty\) (the continuum regime). After block-spin renormalization the effective coupling on scale \(2^k\) behaves as \(\beta_k\sim\beta+c\log 2^k\), so the polymer activities cease to be small precisely when the ultraviolet cutoff is removed. Any attempt to extract a positive mass gap \(m_\text{phys}>0\) independent of the lattice spacing \(a=2^{-k}\) must therefore control an infinite sequence of relevant and marginal operators generated by the RG flow; the lemma supplies no such control. In particular, the constant \(m\) obtained above vanishes as \(\beta\to\infty\), and no quantitative lower bound on the continuum gap follows.

Thus the strong-coupling mass gap is rigorous, yet the only known expansion that produces it is incompatible with the continuum limit required by the Millennium problem.

VERDICT: PARTIAL
REASON: Strong-coupling exponential decay of Wilson correlators proved; continuum RG obstruction identified, no full gap.
