## Attempt: Structural analysis of Li's criterion and the arithmetic obstruction to positivity

I focus on Li's criterion, derive its arithmetic form self-containedly, and
isolate precisely *where* a potential proof of positivity must grapple with
the prime-power sum — identifying the genuine obstruction rather than hiding it.

### Setup

Let $\xi(s) = \tfrac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)$, entire of order 1,
with $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$, and whose zeros are exactly the nontrivial zeros $\rho$
of $\zeta$. Define the Li coefficients
$$
\lambda_n = \frac{1}{(n-1)!}\,\frac{d^n}{ds^n}\Big[s^{n-1}\log\xi(s)\Big]_{s=1},
\qquad n\ge 1.
$$

**Li's theorem (I take as known).** RH $\iff \lambda_n\ge 0$ for all $n\ge1$,
and the Hadamard product gives
$$
\lambda_n=\sum_{\rho}\Big[1-\Big(1-\tfrac1\rho\Big)^n\Big],
$$
the sum taken in the symmetric sense (pairing $\rho$ with $1-\rho$).

### Lemma 1 (positivity of the per-zero contribution under RH)

Write $\rho=\tfrac12+i\gamma$. Pairing $\rho$ and $\bar\rho=1-\rho$ (valid on RH),
the combined contribution is
$$
c_n(\gamma)=2-\Big(1-\tfrac1\rho\Big)^n-\Big(1-\tfrac1{\bar\rho}\Big)^n
=2\,\mathrm{Re}\Big[1-\big(1-\tfrac1\rho\big)^n\Big].
$$
On RH, $1/\rho = \overline{\rho}/|\rho|^2 = (\tfrac12 - i\gamma)/(\tfrac14+\gamma^2)$,
so
$$
1-\tfrac1\rho = \frac{\tfrac14+\gamma^2-\tfrac12+i\gamma}{\tfrac14+\gamma^2}
=\frac{(\gamma^2-\tfrac14)+i\gamma}{\gamma^2+\tfrac14}.
$$
Its modulus squared is
$$
\Big|1-\tfrac1\rho\Big|^2=\frac{(\gamma^2-\tfrac14)^2+\gamma^2}{(\gamma^2+\tfrac14)^2}
=\frac{\gamma^4+\tfrac12\gamma^2+\tfrac1{16}}{(\gamma^2+\tfrac14)^2}
=\frac{(\gamma^2+\tfrac14)^2}{(\gamma^2+\tfrac14)^2}=1.
$$
So **on RH every factor $1-1/\rho$ lies on the unit circle.** Writing
$1-1/\rho=e^{i\theta_\rho}$, we get $c_n(\gamma)=2(1-\cos n\theta_\rho)\ge0$.

Hence $\lambda_n=\sum_{\gamma>0} 2(1-\cos n\theta_\rho)\ge 0$ is **manifest** on RH.
This re-derives the easy direction cleanly, and — the point worth flagging —
shows the converse direction is a statement that the $\theta_\rho$ are *real*,
i.e. that $|1-1/\rho|=1$ for all zeros.

### Lemma 2 (the modulus condition is equivalent to RH, per zero)

For a general zero $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$,
$$
\Big|1-\tfrac1\rho\Big|^2-1
=\frac{|\rho-1|^2-|\rho|^2}{|\rho|^2}
=\frac{(\beta-1)^2-\beta^2}{|\rho|^2}
=\frac{1-2\beta}{|\rho|^2}.
$$
Thus $|1-1/\rho|<1\iff\beta>\tfrac12$, $=1\iff\beta=\tfrac12$, $>1\iff\beta<\tfrac12$.

This gives a clean restatement: **RH $\iff$ for every nontrivial zero,
$|1-1/\rho|=1$**, equivalently the Möbius image $w=1-1/\rho$ of the critical
line is the unit circle (indeed $s\mapsto 1-1/s$ maps $\mathrm{Re}\,s=\tfrac12$
to $|w|=1$). This is the geometric heart of why Li's criterion "sees" RH:
positivity of $\lambda_n=\sum_\rho(1-w_\rho^n)$ for all $n$ forces each $w_\rho$
onto the closed unit disk *and* its conjugate-pair reflection onto it too,
which only the circle satisfies.

### Where a proof must do real work (the obstruction)

The arithmetic content of $\lambda_n$ (via the explicit formula, the standard
expansion) is
$$
\lambda_n = \underbrace{\sum_{j=1}^{n}\binom{n}{j}(-1)^{j+1}
\frac{1}{(j-1)!}\,\eta_{j-1}}_{\text{Stieltjes/field terms}}
\;+\;\Big(1-\tfrac{\gamma_{\mathrm{E}}}{2}-\tfrac12\log 4\pi\Big)\text{-type constants},
$$
where the zero side equals an archimedean term plus
$$
-\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{\Lambda(m)}{\sqrt m}\,P_n\!\big(\tfrac{\log m}{?}\big)\cdots
$$
— i.e. $\lambda_n$ is a *difference* of a smoothly growing "trivial/archimedean"
positive piece $\sim \tfrac n2\log n$ and an oscillatory prime-power piece.
Bombieri–Lagarias showed $\lambda_n = \tfrac n2(\log n -1 +\gamma_E) + \text{error}$
on RH; the positivity of the *main term* is unconditional and automatic.

**The genuine obstruction**, made precise by Lemmas 1–2: the entire difficulty
is compressed into showing the oscillatory prime-sum cannot overwhelm the main
term for a single $n$. But a single off-line zero $\rho_0$ (with its reflection)
contributes $2(1-\mathrm{Re}\,w_{\rho_0}^n)$ where $|w_{\rho_0}|\ne1$; since
$|w_{\rho_0}|>1$ on the side $\beta<\tfrac12$, this term grows *geometrically*
in $n$ and would eventually dominate and (through the conjugate pairing, where
$|w|<1$ contributes boundedly while $|w|>1$ contributes $\to-\infty$ in real
part along suitable $n$) drive some $\lambda_n<0$. 

I can make this last step rigorous: suppose $\rho_0=\beta_0+i\gamma_0$ with
$\beta_0<\tfrac12$ (if any off-line zero exists, by the functional equation its
reflection has real part $>\tfrac12$ and conjugate too; take the one with
$\beta<\tfrac12$). Then $w_0=1-1/\rho_0$ has $|w_0|=r>1$. Write $w_0=re^{i\phi}$.
The pair $\{\rho_0,\overline{\rho_0}\}$ contributes
$$
c_n = 2 - 2 r^n\cos(n\phi).
$$
Along a subsequence $n_k$ with $\cos(n_k\phi)\ge \tfrac12$ (which exists by
equidistribution of $n\phi \bmod 2\pi$ when $\phi/\pi$ irrational, or trivially
if rational), $c_{n_k}\le 2 - r^{n_k}\to-\infty$. Meanwhile the remaining zeros
and the main term grow only polynomially ($O(n\log n)$) and the convergence of
$\sum_\rho$ is controlled by $\sum 1/|\rho|^2<\infty$. Hence $\lambda_{n_k}\to-\infty$,
contradicting $\lambda_n\ge0$.

This gives a **clean, self-contained proof of the hard direction of Li's
criterion** (positivity $\Rightarrow$ RH), modulo the standard uniform control
of the tail $\sum_{\rho\ne\rho_0}c_n(\rho)=O(n\log n)$, which is the one piece I
have not fully bounded here (it requires the standard zero-density/convergence
estimate that the geometric blow-up of a single off-line zero dominates the
polynomial aggregate — true but needing the Hadamard bound $N(T)\sim\frac{T}{2\pi}\log T$).

### Honest assessment

- Lemmas 1–2 and the geometric reformulation ($\text{RH}\iff |1-1/\rho|=1\ \forall\rho$)
  are rigorous and complete.
- The hard-direction argument is correct in structure (a single off-line zero
  forces geometric blow-up beating the polynomial main term) but I have **not**
  rigorously bounded the aggregate tail uniformly in $n$, and more importantly
  this only *reproves a known criterion*; it does not resolve the positivity of
  $\lambda_n$ for all $n$, which is equivalent to RH itself.
- No new progress toward actually proving $\lambda_n\ge0$ unconditionally is
  achieved. The archimedean-vs-prime cancellation that would do so is untouched.

I did not make genuine new progress on RH; I clarified and cleanly re-derived
the geometry underlying Li's criterion.
