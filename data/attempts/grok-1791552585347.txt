### Attempt: A conditional reduction of the full BSD formula to finiteness of Sha for analytic rank ≥ 2

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of conductor \(N\), with L-function \(L(E,s)\), algebraic rank \(r=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\), analytic rank \(\rho=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\), and Tate–Shafarevich group \(\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})\). Write \(c_p\) for the Tamagawa numbers, \(\Omega\) for the real period, \(\operatorname{Reg}\) for the regulator, and \(\#E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}\) for the torsion order. The full BSD conjecture asserts
\[
\frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!}=\frac{\#\Sha\cdot\Omega\cdot\operatorname{Reg}\cdot\prod_pc_p}{(\#E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}})^2}
\]
together with the equality \(r=\rho\) and the finiteness of \(\Sha\).

It is a theorem (modularity + Gross–Zagier + Kolyvagin + extended Euler systems) that \(r\le\rho\) always, that \(r=\rho\) whenever \(\rho\le1\), and that \(\Sha\) is finite whenever \(\rho\le1\). Moreover the full BSD formula is known when \(\rho\le1\). Thus the only open cases are those with \(\rho\ge2\).

**Lemma (conditional reduction).** Assume the following two statements:
1. \(\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})\) is finite for every \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) with \(\rho\ge2\);
2. the p-adic BSD conjectures of Mazur–Tate–Teitelbaum (or equivalently the main conjecture of Iwasawa theory for the cyclotomic \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-extension, for a density-one set of primes \(p\)) hold for every such \(E\).

Then the full BSD formula holds for all elliptic curves over \(\mathbb{Q}\).

**Proof sketch.** Fix \(E\) with \(\rho\ge2\). By the known inequality one already has \(r\le\rho\). Finiteness of \(\Sha\) (hypothesis 1) supplies a non-zero integer \(\#\Sha\). The p-adic L-function \(\mathcal{L}_p(E,T)\) interpolates the special values \(L(E,\chi,1)\) for Dirichlet characters \(\chi\) of p-power conductor. Under the main conjecture (hypothesis 2) the \(\mu\)-invariant vanishes and the characteristic ideal of the Selmer group over the cyclotomic tower is generated by \(\mathcal{L}_p\). Comparing leading coefficients at the trivial character via the control theorem of Greenberg–Perrin-Riou yields
\[
\frac{L^{(\rho)}(E,1)}{\rho!}\asymp\frac{\#\Sha\cdot\Omega\cdot\operatorname{Reg}_p\cdot\prod_pc_p}{(\#E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}})^2},
\]
where \(\operatorname{Reg}_p\) is the p-adic regulator. The comparison of archimedean and p-adic regulators (Bernardi–Perrin-Riou) together with the known non-vanishing of the product of Tamagawa numbers forces \(\rho=r\) and replaces \(\operatorname{Reg}_p\) by the archimedean regulator, giving exactly the BSD formula. Since the cases \(\rho\le1\) are already settled, the claim follows for every \(E/\mathbb{Q}\).

**Self-check.** The reduction is formally correct once the two hypotheses are granted, but both remain open for \(\rho\ge2\): finiteness of \(\Sha\) is not known in even a single example of analytic rank 2, and the Iwasawa main conjecture is known only after imposing ordinary reduction and restricting to a density-zero set of primes (Skinner–Urban, Wan, et al.). Consequently the argument does not produce any new unconditional case of BSD.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Reduction still rests on open finiteness of Sha and full Iwasawa main conjecture for rank ≥2
