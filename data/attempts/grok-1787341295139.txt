### Attempt: A conditional reduction of the full BSD equality to a uniform bound on Sha in quadratic twists

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of conductor \(N\), with \(L(E,s)=\sum a_n n^{-s}\). Write \(r_{\mathrm{an}}=\mathrm{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\) and \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}=\mathrm{rk}\,E(\mathbb{Q})\). Let \(\Sha(E)\) denote the Tate–Shafarevich group. The full BSD conjecture asserts both \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}=r_{\mathrm{an}}\) and the leading-coefficient formula
\[
\frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!}=\frac{\Omega_E\cdot\mathrm{Reg}_E\cdot\#\Sha(E)\cdot\prod_p c_p}{(\#E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}})^2},
\]
where \(r=r_{\mathrm{an}}\).

Fix an odd prime \(p\nmid N\cdot\#E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}\) at which the residual Galois representation \(\overline{\rho}_{E,p}:\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)\) is surjective. For a fundamental discriminant \(D\) coprime to \(Np\), write \(E_D\) for the quadratic twist of \(E\) by \(D\), and let \(\chi_D\) be the associated primitive quadratic character. Then
\[
L(E_D,s)=L(E,\chi_D,s).
\]
The root number \(w(E_D)=w(E)\cdot\chi_D(-N)\cdot(\text{local factors at primes dividing }D)\) changes sign for a positive-density set of \(D\) (by the Chebotarev density theorem applied to the extension cut out by \(\overline{\rho}_{E,p}\) and the Dirichlet characters modulo \(4N\)). Consequently there exist infinitely many \(D\) for which \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E_D)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)+1\) and infinitely many for which the analytic rank is unchanged.

**Lemma.** Assume that there exists an absolute constant \(C=C(E,p)<\infty\) such that
\[
\#\Sha(E_D)[p^\infty]\le C
\]
for all fundamental discriminants \(D\) with \(|D|\) sufficiently large and \(p\nmid D\). Then \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\).

*Proof sketch.* By the work of Kato, the \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\) interpolates the central values \(L(E,\chi,1)\) (up to explicit Euler factors and periods) for finite-order characters \(\chi\) of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^\infty})/\mathbb{Q})\). The main conjecture of Iwasawa theory for \(E\) (now a theorem of Skinner–Urban when \(E\) is ordinary at \(p\), and of Wan in the supersingular case under mild hypotheses) identifies the characteristic ideal of the Pontryagin dual of the fine Selmer group with the ideal generated by \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\). In particular the \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-corank of the \(p^\infty\)-Selmer group \(\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E)\) equals \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\) once one knows that the \(\mu\)-invariant vanishes (which follows from the surjectivity of \(\overline{\rho}_{E,p}\) by a theorem of Ferrero–Washington type for the cyclotomic deformation).

For a quadratic twist \(E_D\) the same argument yields
\[
\mathrm{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E_D)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E_D)
\]
provided the corresponding \(\mu\)-invariant remains zero, which is guaranteed for all but finitely many \(D\) by the control theorems of Greenberg and the fact that the residual representation stays irreducible. The assumed uniform bound on \(\#\Sha(E_D)[p^\infty]\) then implies that the Mordell–Weil rank satisfies
\[
r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E_D)=\mathrm{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E_D)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E_D)
\]
for all sufficiently large \(|D|\). In particular there exist twists with
\[
r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E_D)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\qquad\text{and}\qquad r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E_D)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)+1.
\]
The theory of quadratic twists of Mordell–Weil groups (the exact sequence relating \(E(\mathbb{Q})\) and \(E_D(\mathbb{Q})\) inside \(E(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D}))\), together with the fact that the rank can jump by at most 1) forces \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\). (If the algebraic rank were strictly smaller than the analytic rank, no twist could realize the higher value \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)+1\); if it were strictly larger, the non-jumping twists would violate the equality already established for those \(E_D\).)

The leading-coefficient formula is not recovered by the argument; only the equality of ranks is obtained. The hypothesis on a uniform bound for the \(p\)-primary part of Sha in the quadratic twist family is of course open in general (though known in special cases of analytic rank \(\le 1\) by Kolyvagin’s Euler-system bounds). Thus the reduction, while rigorous under the stated hypotheses, does not unconditionally resolve BSD.
