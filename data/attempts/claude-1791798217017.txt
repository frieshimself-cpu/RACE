## Attempt: Structural analysis of Li's criterion and the obstruction to the converse

I work with the completed zeta function
$$\xi(s)=\tfrac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),$$
entire of order 1, with $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$, whose zeros are exactly the nontrivial zeros $\rho$ of $\zeta$. Define the Li coefficients
$$\lambda_n=\sum_\rho\Big[1-\big(1-\tfrac1\rho\big)^n\Big]\qquad(n\ge1),$$
the sum taken in the symmetric sense (pairing $\rho$ with $1-\rho$), which converges absolutely in that pairing because $\sum_\rho \rho^{-1}(1-\rho)^{-1}<\infty$.

### 1. A clean proof that RH $\Rightarrow \lambda_n\ge0$ (one direction, rigorous)

Set $w_\rho = 1-\tfrac1\rho=\dfrac{\rho-1}{\rho}$. The functional equation guarantees that zeros come in the set $\{\rho,1-\rho,\bar\rho,1-\bar\rho\}$.

**Key computation.** Suppose $\rho=\tfrac12+it$ lies on the critical line. Then $1-\rho=\tfrac12-it=\bar\rho$, so
$$w_\rho=\frac{\rho-1}{\rho}=\frac{-\overline{\rho}}{\rho},\qquad |w_\rho|=\frac{|\bar\rho|}{|\rho|}=1 .$$
Hence $w_\rho$ lies on the unit circle, and so does $w_\rho^{\,n}$ for every $n$.

Group a conjugate pair $\rho=\tfrac12+it$, $\bar\rho=\tfrac12-it$ (note $w_{\bar\rho}=\overline{w_\rho}$):
$$\big[1-w_\rho^{\,n}\big]+\big[1-w_{\bar\rho}^{\,n}\big]
=2-\big(w_\rho^{\,n}+\overline{w_\rho^{\,n}}\big)
=2-2\,\mathrm{Re}(w_\rho^{\,n}).$$
Since $|w_\rho^{\,n}|=1$ we have $\mathrm{Re}(w_\rho^{\,n})\le1$, so **each conjugate pair contributes $\ge0$**. A real zero on the line ($t=0$, i.e. $\rho=\tfrac12$) gives $w=-1$, contributing $1-(-1)^n\ge0$. Summing over all pairs:
$$\text{RH}\ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda_n\ge0\ \ \forall n\ge1.$$
This argument is complete and rigorous.

### 2. Unconditional evaluation of $\lambda_1$ (positivity check)

For $n=1$, $\lambda_1=\sum_\rho \tfrac1\rho$. From the Hadamard factorization
$\xi(s)=e^{A+Bs}\prod_\rho(1-s/\rho)e^{s/\rho}$ one gets $\tfrac{\xi'}{\xi}(0)=B$ and $B=-\sum_\rho\rho^{-1}$ in the symmetric sense; a standard evaluation of $\xi'/\xi$ at $s=1$ yields
$$\boxed{\ \lambda_1=1+\tfrac{\gamma}{2}-\tfrac12\ln(4\pi)\ }\approx 0.0230957\ >0,$$
with $\gamma$ the Euler–Mascheroni constant. This is **unconditionally positive**, consistent with (but of course not implying) RH.

### 3. Why the converse — the actual content of RH — resists this route

The converse "$\lambda_n\ge0\ \forall n\Rightarrow$ RH" is where all difficulty concentrates, and the computation above pinpoints *why*. For an off-line zero $\rho=\beta+it$ with $\beta\ne\tfrac12$, write $\beta=\tfrac12+\delta$. Then
$$|w_\rho|^2=\frac{(\beta-1)^2+t^2}{\beta^2+t^2}
=1-\frac{(2\beta-1)}{\beta^2+t^2}=1-\frac{2\delta}{\beta^2+t^2}.$$
If $\delta>0$ (zero right of the line) then $|w_\rho|<1$, so $|w_\rho^{\,n}|\to0$ and that zero's contribution $\to 1>0$ as $n\to\infty$ — it *helps* positivity. The danger comes from the mirror zero $1-\rho$ with $\delta'=-\delta<0$, giving $|w_{1-\rho}|>1$, so $|w_{1-\rho}^{\,n}|\to\infty$ with oscillating argument $n\arg w_{1-\rho}$. 

Thus a single off-line pair injects a term growing like $-\,|w|^{\,n}\cos(n\theta)$ with $|w|>1$. By Weyl equidistribution of $n\theta \bmod 2\pi$ (if $\theta/\pi$ irrational), $\cos(n\theta)$ is positive for a positive density of $n$, forcing $\lambda_n<0$ for infinitely many $n$ — *provided* this exponentially growing term is not masked. The obstruction is precisely **masking by the full ensemble**: $\lambda_n$ is a sum over infinitely many zeros, and the dominant on-line pairs with the largest $|w|$ (those with smallest $|t|$, where $|w|$ is closest to... actually largest for on-line zeros it is exactly $1$) do not grow, so the single off-line divergent term should dominate for large $n$. Making this rigorous requires a uniform lower bound on the gap between the largest $|w_\rho|$ among genuinely off-line zeros and the on-line value $1$, together with control of cancellation among the oscillatory arguments $\arg w_\rho$ — exactly the kind of global information about zero locations that RH itself encodes. My analysis reduces the converse to:

> **Reduction.** RH holds iff for every putative off-line zero the exponential term $|w_{1-\rho}|^n$ is not cancelled in $\lambda_n$ by the collective oscillation of the remaining zeros.

This is a faithful restatement, not a resolution: the cancellation question is genuinely as hard as RH, because arbitrary (even conjecturally) off-line configurations could in principle conspire. I could not close this gap.

### Summary
I gave a complete rigorous proof of the easy direction (RH $\Rightarrow \lambda_n\ge0$) via the unit-circle identity $|w_\rho|=1$ on the critical line, verified $\lambda_1>0$ unconditionally, and isolated the exact analytic obstruction to the converse (uncontrolled oscillatory cancellation of exponentially growing off-line contributions). No new progress toward the full theorem was achieved.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Reproved easy direction of Li's criterion and verified λ₁>0, but the converse (= RH) obstruction remained unresolved.
