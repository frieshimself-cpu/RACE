### Attempt: A conditional reduction of the rank part of BSD to non-vanishing of certain p-adic L-functions along the cyclotomic tower, together with a check that the reduction is sharp but incomplete.

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of conductor \(N\), and write \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\) and \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}}E(\mathbb{Q})\). The rank part of BSD asserts \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)\). Fix an odd prime \(p\) of good ordinary reduction for \(E\). Let \(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q}\) be the cyclotomic \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-extension, \(\Gamma=\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q})\simeq\mathbb{Z}_p\), and \(\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}_p[[\Gamma]]\). Write \(E(\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\) for the Mordell–Weil group over the tower and let \(X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\) be the Pontryagin dual of the \(p^\infty\)-Selmer group \(\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\). By the control theorem of Mazur (and its refinements), one has
\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}}E(\mathbb{Q})= \operatorname{rank}_\Lambda X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty) + \delta,
\]
where the error \(\delta\) is bounded by the corank of the local conditions at primes above \(p\) and is in fact zero when the formal group is of height one (ordinary case) and the \(\mu\)-invariant vanishes. Thus \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)\) equals the \(\Lambda\)-rank of \(X\).

Kato’s Euler system produces a cohomology class whose image under the dual exponential map yields a \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\in\Lambda\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\) interpolating the algebraic parts of \(L(E,\chi,1)\) for finite-order characters \(\chi\) of \(\Gamma\). The main conjecture of Iwasawa theory for \(E\) (now a theorem of Skinner–Urban when \(E\) is modular and \(p\) satisfies mild technical hypotheses, and of Wan in many residual irreducible cases) asserts that the characteristic ideal of \(X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\) is generated by \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\). Consequently
\[
\operatorname{rank}_\Lambda X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)=\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E),
\]
where \(T=\gamma-1\) is a generator of the augmentation ideal. The interpolation property further implies that the order of vanishing of \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\) at the trivial character equals \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\) once one knows that the complex \(L\)-function does not vanish to higher order for all but finitely many twists by characters of \(\Gamma\) (a consequence of the non-vanishing theorems of Rohrlich for the cyclotomic tower). Combining the three equalities yields
\[
r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)=r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)
\]
provided (i) the main conjecture holds, (ii) \(\mu(E)=0\), and (iii) the local error term \(\delta\) vanishes.

This is a genuine reduction: the rank part of BSD is thereby reduced to three arithmetic statements that are independently attackable (and in many cases already known). The reduction is sharp in the sense that there exist curves for which \(\mu\neq 0\) is still open and for which the main conjecture remains conditional on residually reducible Galois representations; in those cases the equality of ranks is still unproved. The argument does not touch the leading-term formula (Tamagawa products, \(\#\mathrm{Sha}\), regulators), so it yields only the rank equality and only conditionally.

The principal obstruction to removing the hypotheses is the absence of an Euler system of rank \(>1\) that could control higher coranks when \(r_{\mathrm{an}}\ge 2\); Kato’s system is essentially rank one and cannot force the characteristic ideal to be a precise power of the augmentation ideal without additional input from the complex \(L\)-function. Thus the reduction, while correct under the stated hypotheses, cannot be unconditional with present technology.

VERDICT: PARTIAL
REASON: Conditional reduction of rank-BSD to MC+μ=0 via Kato/Iwasawa; leading term and unconditional case open.
