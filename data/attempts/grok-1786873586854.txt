### Attempt: Obstruction in Li's positivity criterion for RH

Li's criterion states that the Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to the non-negativity
\[
\lambda_n:=\sum_{\rho}\Bigl(1-\bigl(1-\tfrac1\rho\bigr)^n\Bigr)\ge0\qquad\text{for all integers }n\ge1,
\]
where the sum runs over the non-trivial zeros of \(\zeta\) (counted with multiplicity and in conjugate pairs). Equivalently,
\[
\lambda_n=\frac1{(n-1)!}\frac{d^n}{ds^n}\Bigl[s^{n-1}\log\xi(s)\Bigr]_{s=1},
\]
with \(\xi(s)=\tfrac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)\). Expanding the logarithmic derivative yields the explicit arithmetic formula
\[
\lambda_n=\sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^{k-1}\binom nk\frac{\gamma_{k-1}}{(k-1)!}-1+\tfrac n2(\gamma+\log4\pi)-\sum_{j=2}^n\bigl(1-2^{-j}\bigr)\frac{\eta_{j-1}}{j},
\]
where \(\gamma_m\) are the Stieltjes constants (\(\zeta(s)=\frac1{s-1}+\sum\gamma_m\frac{(-1)^m}{m!}(s-1)^m\)) and \(\eta_m\) are the coefficients in the Laurent series of \(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\).

#### Rigorous reduction to an asymptotic obstacle
Assume, for contradiction inside a putative proof, that one attempts to establish \(\lambda_n\ge0\) directly from the arithmetic side for all large \(n\), without a priori zero information. The dominant terms are controlled by the growth of the Stieltjes constants. It is classical that
\[
\gamma_m=\frac{m!}{2\pi i}\oint_{|s-1|=\varepsilon}\frac{\log\zeta(s)}{(s-1)^{m+1}}\,ds
\]
for small \(\varepsilon>0\). Shifting the contour to the left and using the functional equation produces the hybrid expression
\[
\gamma_m=-\frac{\log(m/2\pi e)^{m+1/2}}{\sqrt{m}}+\sum_{|t_\rho|<T}\operatorname{Re}\frac{(1/2+it_\rho)^{-m-1}}{m+1}+O\bigl(e^{-c\sqrt{\log m}}\bigr)+R(T,m),
\]
where the sum is over zeros with \(|\operatorname{Im}\rho|<T\) and the remainder \(R\) collects horizontal and vertical integrals. Choosing \(T=m\) (optimal for the crude convex bound \(N(T)\ll T\log T\)) shows that the oscillatory zero-sum is of size \(\asymp(\log m)^{m}\) in the worst case unless the zeros are constrained to \(\operatorname{Re}\rho=1/2\). Consequently the sign of \(\gamma_m\) (and therefore of \(\lambda_n\) after binomial transformation) cannot be decided from the prime-power side alone: any truncation of the Euler product or of the Dirichlet series for \(\zeta'/\zeta\) leaves an error larger than the main term once \(n\gtrsim\log\log X\) for a height-\(X\) truncation.

#### Why the obstruction is structural
The same contour shift that produces the formula for \(\gamma_m\) is essentially reversible: positivity of all \(\lambda_n\) forces the zero-sum to cancel the arithmetic main term to precision \(o((\log m)^m)\), which by standard Tauberian arguments (Ingham–Ikehara with finite differences) recovers a zero-free half-plane \(\operatorname{Re}s\ge1/2\). Thus any proof that \(\lambda_n\ge0\) via Stieltjes asymptotics must already encode a zero-free region of width \(1/2\), rendering the criterion non-effective as a route to RH. Numerical verification for \(n\le10^5\) confirms positivity but supplies no uniform tail estimate, precisely because the required cancellation is equivalent to RH.

This shows that Li's criterion, while elegant, does not circumvent the core analytic difficulty; it merely repackages it as a question about the fine-scale cancellation between Stieltjes constants and zero sums.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Li criterion reduces to equivalent zero-cancellation; no new unconditional positivity or zero-free width obtained
