### Attempt: A reduction of the parity conjecture for BSD via local root numbers and 2-descent

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of conductor \(N\). Write \(r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=\mathrm{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\) and \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)=\mathrm{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}}E(\mathbb{Q})\). The parity conjecture (a weak form of BSD) asserts
\[
r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\equiv r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)\pmod{2}.
\]
It is known unconditionally that the global root number \(w(E)=(-1)^{r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)}\). Thus parity is equivalent to \(w(E)=(-1)^{r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)}\).

We reduce the parity conjecture, for curves with a rational 2-torsion point, to a purely local statement about the Cassels–Tate pairing on the 2-Selmer group after a quadratic twist.

#### Setup
Assume \(E(\mathbb{Q})[2]\simeq\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), generated by a point \(T\). Let \(\phi:E\to E'\) be the associated isogeny of degree 2 (kernel \(\{O,T\}\)) and \(\hat\phi\) its dual. The 2-Selmer group \(\mathrm{Sel}_2(E/\mathbb{Q})\) sits in the exact sequence
\[
0\to E(\mathbb{Q})/2E(\mathbb{Q})\to\mathrm{Sel}_2(E/\mathbb{Q})\to\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})[2]\to0.
\]
The Cassels–Tate pairing induces a non-degenerate alternating form on \(\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})[2^\infty]/\mathrm{div}\) (assuming the latter is finite, which we do not yet know). Restricting to the \(\phi\)-Selmer groups yields the usual parity relation
\[
\dim_{\mathbb{F}_2}\mathrm{Sel}_\phi(E/\mathbb{Q})-\dim_{\mathbb{F}_2}\mathrm{Sel}_{\hat\phi}(E'/\mathbb{Q})\equiv r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)\pmod{2},
\]
up to the contribution of the local images of the Kummer maps at places of bad reduction and at 2.

#### Local root-number identity
For each place \(v\) write \(w_v(E)\) for the local root number, so \(w(E)=\prod_v w_v(E)\). When \(v\nmid 2\infty\) and \(E\) has multiplicative reduction, \(w_v(E)=-1\) precisely when the Tamagawa number \(c_v(E)\) is even. For the isogenous curve the product of Tamagawa ratios satisfies
\[
\prod_v\frac{c_v(E)}{c_v(E')}=\frac{\#E'(\mathbb{Q})[\hat\phi]}{\#E(\mathbb{Q})[\phi]}\in\{1,2\}.
\]
A direct computation with the local Galois cohomology groups \(H^1(\mathbb{Q}_v,E[\phi])\) shows that the dimension of the local Kummer image differs from \(\frac12(1-w_v(E))\) by at most the contribution of the formal group at \(v=2\). Consequently
\[
\dim_{\mathbb{F}_2}\mathrm{Sel}_\phi(E/\mathbb{Q})\equiv\sum_v\frac{1-w_v(E)}{2}\pmod{2},
\]
provided the 2-adic place contributes evenly (i.e., the formal group of \(E\) over \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) has even valuation of the discriminant or the curve is ordinary at 2). The right-hand side is exactly \(\frac12(1-w(E))\) modulo 2. Combining with the global Selmer parity therefore yields
\[
r_{\mathrm{alg}}(E)\equiv r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\pmod{2}
\]
as soon as the local condition at 2 holds and \(\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})[2^\infty]\) is finite (so that the Cassels–Tate pairing is non-degenerate).

#### Remaining obstruction
The argument fails to be unconditional for two reasons:
1. Finiteness of \(\Sha[2^\infty]\) is not known in general (it is known only when \(r_{\mathrm{an}}\le1\) by Kolyvagin).
2. When \(E\) is supersingular at 2 the local Euler factor and the image of the Kummer map in \(H^1(\mathbb{Q}_2,E[2])\) can differ by an odd-dimensional space; the root-number identity then acquires an extra sign that cannot be cancelled by Tamagawa numbers alone.

Thus the reduction establishes the parity conjecture for all \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) possessing a rational 2-torsion point, good ordinary reduction at 2, and finite \(\Sha[2^\infty]\). Removing the finiteness hypothesis would require a 2-adic analogue of the Gross–Zagier formula controlling the \(\phi\)-Selmer corank directly from the derivative of a 2-adic \(L\)-function; no such formula is presently available when the analytic rank exceeds 1.

This is therefore only a conditional partial result: parity (hence the mod-2 part of BSD) holds under the listed local and Sha-finiteness hypotheses, but the general case remains open precisely because those hypotheses are not known to hold universally.
