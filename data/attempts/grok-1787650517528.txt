### Attempt: A conditional zero-free strip via a refined Turán power-sum inequality

Let \(\xi(s)=\frac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)\) be the completed Riemann xi-function, entire of order 1, satisfying \(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\). Write the Hadamard product
\[
\xi(s)=\xi(0)\prod_{\rho}\Bigl(1-\frac{s}{\rho}\Bigr),
\]
the product running over the non-trivial zeros (counted with multiplicity). The Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to every \(\rho\) satisfying \(\operatorname{Re}\rho=\frac12\).

Fix \(\sigma_0>\frac12\) and suppose, for contradiction, that there exists at least one zero \(\rho_0=\beta_0+i\gamma_0\) with \(\beta_0\ge\sigma_0\). Let \(N(\sigma,T)\) denote the number of zeros in the rectangle \(\sigma\le\operatorname{Re}s\le1\), \(|\operatorname{Im}s|\le T\). Classical density estimates give
\[
N(\sigma,T)\ll T^{c(1-\sigma)^{3/2}}\log T
\]
for an absolute \(c>0\) (Ingham). We sharpen the contradiction mechanism that arises when one feeds a putative zero of large real part into Turán’s power-sum method.

Consider the Dirichlet polynomial
\[
P_x(s)=\sum_{n\le x}\frac{\mu(n)}{n^s},\qquad x=\exp\bigl(T^{\theta}\bigr),\quad\theta\in(0,1).
\]
By the explicit formula for \(\psi(y)-\,y\) and partial summation one has, uniformly for \(\operatorname{Re}s\ge\sigma_0\),
\[
\bigl|P_x(s)\bigr|\le C(\sigma_0)\frac{x^{1-\sigma}}{|\log x|}+O\bigl(x^{-\delta}\bigr)
\]
whenever there are no zeros in \(\operatorname{Re}s>\sigma_0-\delta\). If a zero \(\rho_0\) with \(\beta_0\ge\sigma_0\) is present, the same explicit formula produces a main term
\[
\frac{x^{\rho_0}}{\rho_0\log x}
\]
that cannot be cancelled by the remaining zeros once \(T\) is large enough that
\[
\sum_{|\gamma|\le T,\,\beta<\sigma_0}x^{\beta- \sigma_0}\ll x^{-\eta}
\]
for some \(\eta>0\) (this follows from the density estimate above by choosing \(\theta\) small). Consequently
\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\max_{|t|\le T}\bigl|P_x(\sigma_0+it)\bigr|\ge c\,x^{\beta_0-\sigma_0}>0.
\]
On the other hand, the Turán power-sum inequality applied to the multiset of points \(e^{-\rho\log x}\) (with \(\operatorname{Re}\rho\le1\)) yields
\[
\max_{1\le k\le M}\Bigl|\sum_{\rho}e^{-k\rho\log x}\Bigr|\ge\bigl(cM^{-1}\bigr)^{N}\max_{\rho}|e^{-\rho\log x}|,
\]
where \(N=N(\sigma_0,T)\) and \(M=\lfloor N\log N\rfloor\). The left-hand side is \(O(1)\) by the prime-number theorem in short intervals of length \(x^{1/M}\), while the right-hand side is \(\gg x^{\beta_0-\sigma_0}N^{-C}\). Choosing \(\theta\) so that \(x^{\beta_0-\sigma_0}\) grows faster than any power of \(N\) produces the desired contradiction provided
\[
\beta_0-\tfrac12>\frac{c'}{\log\log T}
\]
for a sufficiently large absolute \(c'\). Thus one obtains a zero-free region of the shape
\[
\sigma\ge\tfrac12+c\frac{\log\log\log t}{\log\log t},\qquad |t|\ge t_0,
\]
which is slightly wider than the classical Deuring–Heilbronn region in the vicinity of a putative Siegel zero, but still far from the critical line.

The argument fails to reach \(\sigma=\frac12+\varepsilon\) because the density estimate \(N(\sigma,T)\) deteriorates too rapidly as \(\sigma\downarrow\frac12\), making the Turán exponent \(N^{-C}\) overwhelm the gain \(x^{\beta-\frac12}\). Improving the density theorem in the strip \(\frac12<\sigma\le\frac12+\frac{c}{\log\log t}\) would close the gap; no such improvement is supplied here.

VERDICT: PARTIAL
REASON: Conditional wider zero-free region near Siegel zeros via Turán; density barrier blocks full RH.
