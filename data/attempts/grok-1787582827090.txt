### Attempt: A conditional reduction of the rank part of BSD via truncated Euler products and effective Chebotarev

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of conductor \(N\), with \(L(E,s)=\sum a_n n^{-s}\) its Hasse–Weil \(L\)-function (normalized so the critical point is \(s=1\)). Write \(r_{\mathrm{an}}=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\) and \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\). The rank part of BSD asserts \(r_{\mathrm{an}}=r_{\mathrm{alg}}\). We work throughout with the incomplete Euler product
\[
P_x(s)=\prod_{p\le x}(1-a_pp^{-s}+p^{1-2s})^{-1}
\]
and the associated logarithmic derivative
\[
\frac{P_x'}{P_x}(s)=-\sum_{p\le x}\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{a_{p^k}\log p}{p^{ks}}.
\]
It is classical that \(L(E,s)\) admits an analytic continuation and functional equation, so the Taylor expansion
\[
L(E,s)=c_r(s-1)^r+O(|s-1|^{r+1}),\qquad r=r_{\mathrm{an}},\quad c_r\neq0,
\]
is valid in a neighbourhood of \(s=1\). Our goal is a reduction showing that if a certain effective Chebotarev density statement holds for the division fields of \(E\), then \(r_{\mathrm{an}}\ge r_{\mathrm{alg}}\) follows from the non-vanishing of a finite Euler factor correction; the opposite inequality is already known in many cases by Kolyvagin–Gross–Zagier when Heegner hypotheses are satisfied, so the reduction would settle the rank equality conditionally.

#### Setup and Selmer control
Let \(p\) be an odd prime of good ordinary reduction for \(E\), and let \(\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})\) be the \(p^\infty\)-Selmer group. Write
\[
\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=r_{\mathrm{alg}}+\delta_p,
\]
where \(\delta_p\ge0\) measures the contribution of the \(p\)-primary part of \(\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})\) (assumed cotorsion, as is known after Kato for the cyclotomic deformation). By the control theorem of Mazur, for all sufficiently large \(n\) the Kummer map realises
\[
E(\mathbb{Q})/p^nE(\mathbb{Q})\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Sel}_{p^n}(E/\mathbb{Q})
\]
with cokernel bounded independently of \(n\). Consequently any upper bound on the growth of partial Euler products that forces \(r_{\mathrm{an}}<r_{\mathrm{alg}}+\delta_p\) would contradict the existence of \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}\) independent global points, once \(\delta_p\) is controlled.

#### Truncated product and zero detection
Define the partial sum of the logarithmic derivative at the critical point by
\[
S(x):=-\frac{P_x'}{P_x}(1)=\sum_{p\le x}\frac{a_p\log p}{p}+O\bigl(x^{1/2+\varepsilon}\bigr).
\]
A standard tauberian argument (Wiener–Ikehara applied to the Dirichlet series of \(a_p\log p\), justified by the known zero-free region of \(L(E,s)\) to the right of \(\operatorname{Re}s=1\)) yields
\[
S(x)=r_{\mathrm{an}}\log x+C_E+o(1)
\]
as \(x\to\infty\), where \(C_E\) is an effective constant depending only on the conductor and the residue of \(L\) at \(s=1\) (or the leading Taylor coefficient when \(r_{\mathrm{an}}>0\)).

On the arithmetic side, each global point \(P\in E(\mathbb{Q})\) of infinite order produces, via its reduction modulo primes, a bias in the traces \(a_p\). More precisely, if \(\{P_1,\dots,P_r\}\) is a \(\mathbb{Z}\)-basis of \(E(\mathbb{Q})/E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}\) with \(r=r_{\mathrm{alg}}\), the fields \(\mathbb{Q}(E[m],\frac1m P_i)\) are Galois extensions whose Frobenius conjugacy classes are constrained by the Néron–Tate heights. Effective Chebotarev (in the form of Lagarias–Odlyzko under GRH for Artin \(L\)-functions of these extensions, or unconditionally with a weaker error via Serre’s version) supplies a positive-density set of primes \(p\le x\) for which
\[
a_p\equiv2\pmod{m}\qquad\text{or}\qquad a_p\equiv\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{Frob}_p|_{T_\ell E})\in\{-1,0,1\}
\]
according to the splitting of the \(P_i\). Summing the resulting discrepancies gives the lower bound
\[
S(x)\ge r_{\mathrm{alg}}\log x-C_E'-O\bigl(x^{\theta}\bigr)
\]
for an absolute \(\theta<1\) (the GRH case yields \(\theta=1/2+\varepsilon\); the unconditional case forces a larger but still \(o(\log x)\) error after partial summation).

#### The reduction
Comparing the two asymptotic expansions for \(S(x)\), we obtain
\[
r_{\mathrm{an}}\ge r_{\mathrm{alg}}
\]
as soon as the error terms are smaller than \(\frac12\log x\), which is guaranteed once the effective Chebotarev constants for the division fields \(\mathbb{Q}(E[m],\frac1m P_i)\) (with \(m\) running over a density-one set of integers) are bounded by a power of the discriminant smaller than any positive power of \(x\). The only obstruction is therefore the size of the Artin conductors of these extensions, which grow like \(O(m^{O(r_{\mathrm{alg}})}N^{O(1)})\). If one assumes a uniform bound
\[
\operatorname{cond}(\mathbb{Q}(E[m],P_i/m))\ll_{\varepsilon} m^{2+\varepsilon}N^{C}
\]
(with \(C\) absolute), the error is admissible and the inequality follows. (The reverse inequality \(r_{\mathrm{an}}\le r_{\mathrm{alg}}\) is not addressed by this method; it would require a matching upper bound on \(S(x)\) coming from the non-existence of extra Selmer classes, i.e., finiteness of \(\Sha[p^\infty]\).)

#### Where the argument fails to be unconditional
The required conductor bound is false in general: the division fields can have Galois groups containing large open subgroups of \(\operatorname{GSp}_{2r}(\mathbb{Z}/m)\), whose conductors grow like \(m^{O(r^2)}\). Without an independent upper bound on \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}\) (circular) or a replacement for effective Chebotarev that avoids GRH and large conductors, the error term \(O(x^\theta)\) cannot be kept smaller than \(\log x\). Thus the comparison of asymptotics does not close. The same obstruction appears if one tries to replace ordinary primes by supersingular primes or to work with the \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function: the \(\mu\)-invariant control still demands a Chebotarev input of comparable strength.

Consequently the reduction, while correctly identifying that BSD-rank is implied by a sufficiently strong effective Chebotarev statement for the fields cut out by global points, does not remove the circularity and yields no new unconditional case.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Chebotarev conductor growth blocks error control; reduction circular without prior rank bound
