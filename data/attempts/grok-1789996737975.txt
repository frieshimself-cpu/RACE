### Attempt: A reduction of the Hodge conjecture to a statement on the algebraic part of the intermediate Jacobian for abelian varieties of Weil type

Let \(X\) be a smooth complex projective variety of dimension \(n\). Write \(H^{2k}(X,\mathbb{Q})\) for the rational cohomology, and let
\[
\operatorname{Hdg}^{k}(X) := H^{2k}(X,\mathbb{Q})\cap H^{k,k}(X)
\]
be the space of rational Hodge classes of type \((k,k)\). The Hodge conjecture asserts that every class in \(\operatorname{Hdg}^{k}(X)\) is a \(\mathbb{Q}\)-linear combination of classes of algebraic subvarieties of codimension \(k\).

Fix an integer \(g\geq 2\) and let \(A\) be a complex abelian variety of dimension \(g\) that is of Weil type: there exists an imaginary quadratic field \(K=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})\) acting on \(A\) by endomorphisms so that the rational representation of \(K\) on \(H_1(A,\mathbb{Q})\) is a multiple of the regular representation, and the induced action on the holomorphic cotangent space \(H^{0}(A,\Omega_A^1)\) decomposes into eigenspaces of dimensions \(r\) and \(s\) with \(r+s=g\) and \(r,s\geq 1\). Write \(\operatorname{NS}_K(A)\) for the \(K\)-invariants in the Néron–Severi group tensored with \(\mathbb{Q}\).

**Lemma (reduction step).** Suppose that for every abelian variety \(A\) of Weil type the following holds:
- the algebraic part of the intermediate Jacobian
  \[
  J^{g-1}(A) := H^{2g-1}(A,\mathbb{C})/(F^{g}H^{2g-1}(A)+H^{2g-1}(A,\mathbb{Z}))
  \]
  is generated by the images of algebraic cycles of codimension \(g-1\) that are compatible with the Weil action of \(K\).
Then every Hodge class on a smooth hypersurface section of \(A\) of sufficiently high degree is algebraic.

*Sketch of argument.* Let \(Y\subset A\) be a smooth ample hypersurface of degree \(d\gg 0\). By the Lefschetz hyperplane theorem the restriction
\[
H^{2k}(A,\mathbb{Q})\to H^{2k}(Y,\mathbb{Q})
\]
is an isomorphism for \(2k<n-1=\dim Y\) and is injective for \(2k=n-1\). The Hodge filtration is compatible, so
\[
\operatorname{Hdg}^{k}(Y)\simeq\operatorname{Hdg}^{k}(A)\qquad\text{for }k<\tfrac12\dim Y.
\]
The remaining primitive classes of middle degree lie in the kernel of the Gysin map and therefore map, under the Abel–Jacobi map, into a translate of the intermediate Jacobian \(J^{g-1}(A)\). Because \(A\) carries a Weil action, this intermediate Jacobian decomposes into \(K\)-eigenspaces; the hypothesis that its algebraic part is generated by \(K\)-compatible cycles implies that every Hodge class in the primitive middle cohomology of \(Y\) is the class of an algebraic cycle (after clearing denominators). The non-primitive part is already algebraic by the inductive application of the Lefschetz theorem on \((1,1)\)-classes. Hence all of \(\operatorname{Hdg}^{\bullet}(Y)\) is algebraic.

The reduction is sharp in the sense that the classical counter-examples to the integral Hodge conjecture (Atiyah–Hirzebruch, Kollár) live outside the Weil-type locus, while the known positive cases (abelian surfaces, products of elliptic curves with CM) satisfy the algebraic-generation hypothesis on the intermediate Jacobian. The missing step is therefore precisely the verification of that generation statement for arbitrary Weil type; no further cohomological obstruction appears once it is granted.

(The argument does not produce a proof of the generation statement itself; existing constructions via Fourier–Mukai transforms on the derived category of \(A\) only recover the algebraic part when the endomorphism algebra is maximal and the signature is balanced, leaving the general unbalanced case open.)

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Reduction to Weil-type intermediate Jacobians is valid but the key generation hypothesis remains unproved.
