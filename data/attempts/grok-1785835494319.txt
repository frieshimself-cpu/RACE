### Attempt: A Conditional Reduction via Weighted Pair Correlation and a Failed Positivity Closure

Let \(\xi(s)=\frac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)\) be the completed zeta function (entire of order 1, functional equation \(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\)). Write the non-trivial zeros as \(\rho=\beta+i\gamma\) (with multiplicity) and assume, for contradiction inside a vertical strip, that there exists at least one zero with \(\beta_0>1/2\). Normalize heights so that we work with the measure
\[
d\mu_T(t)=\frac{1}{N(T)}\sum_{|\gamma|\le T}\delta_{\gamma}(t),\qquad N(T)=\#\{\rho:|\gamma|\le T\}\sim\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}.
\]
Montgomery’s pair correlation conjecture (unconditionally known in a restricted range by work of Rudnick–Sarnak and others for the GUE form factor) asserts that for test functions \(f\) whose Fourier transform \(\hat f\) is supported in \((-1,1)\),
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)\,d\mu_T^{(2)}(t)\to\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)\Bigl(1-\Bigl(\frac{\sin\pi t}{\pi t}\Bigr)^2\Bigr)dt
\]
as \(T\to\infty\), where \(d\mu_T^{(2)}\) is the second empirical measure on differences \(\gamma-\gamma'\).

Now form the weighted quadratic functional (a smoothed variant of the Weil explicit formula)
\[
Q_T(g)=\sum_{\rho,\rho'}\hat g\Bigl(\frac{\gamma-\gamma'}{2\pi}\Bigr)w(\beta)w(\beta')\,e^{-(\gamma^2+\gamma'^2)/T^2},
\]
where \(w(\beta)=(\beta-1/2)^2\) (strictly positive off the critical line) and \(g\) is a fixed even Schwartz function with \(\operatorname{supp}\hat g\subset(-1+\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon)\) and \(\hat g\ge0\). Expanding via the explicit formula, the diagonal contribution is
\[
Q_T^{\mathrm{diag}}(g)=\Bigl(\int w(\beta)\,d\mu_T(\beta)\Bigr)\cdot\Bigl(\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}\Bigr)\int\hat g+\text{lower-order arithmetic terms from }\Lambda,
\]
while the off-diagonal contribution is controlled by the pair correlation form factor and is \(O(T\log T)\). The arithmetic side (primes) contributes a main term that is positive and of size \(\asymp T(\log T)^2\int|g|\) precisely when the support condition on \(\hat g\) holds.

If one could pass to the limit and obtain
\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{Q_T(g)}{T\log T}\ge c_g\int w\,d\mu>0
\]
whenever \(\mu\) charges \(\{\beta>1/2\}\), a contradiction would follow because the functional equation forces the total mass of \(w\,d\mu\) to vanish (symmetry \(\beta\leftrightarrow1-\beta\)). The obstruction is the following:

**Lemma (support barrier).**  
For every admissible \(g\) as above, the contribution of zeros with \(\beta\ge1/2+\delta\) is absorbed into an error of size \(O_\delta(T\exp(-c\sqrt{\log T}))\) coming from the zero-density estimates of Huxley type
\[
N(\sigma,T)\ll T^{c(1-\sigma)^{3/2}}\log^{O(1)}T\qquad(\sigma\ge1/2+\delta).
\]
Thus \(Q_T(g)\) remains non-negative (and asymptotically matches the GUE prediction) even if a sparse set of zeros with \(\beta>1/2\) exists, provided their density is smaller than the Huxley bound. The positivity of \(Q_T\) therefore closes only under an a-priori zero-density hypothesis stronger than what is currently known, and does not by itself force \(w\equiv0\).

Consequently the reduction “pair correlation + weighted positivity \(\implies\) RH” is circular: it requires a density estimate already implying that almost all zeros satisfy \(\beta=1/2\), which is a weaker form of the same statement. The approach fails to produce an unconditional contradiction.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Positivity+pair correlation reduction circular; needs density bounds already near RH
