### Attempt: A positivity obstruction for a naive Hilbert–Pólya operator on the critical strip

Let \(\Xi(t)=\xi(\tfrac12+it)\) denote the Riemann \(\xi\)-function restricted to the critical line (entire of order 1, real on \(\mathbb{R}\), and even). The Hilbert–Pólya conjecture posits a self-adjoint operator \(H\) on a Hilbert space \(\mathcal{H}\) whose spectrum is precisely the multiset of ordinates \(\{t_n\}\) of the non-trivial zeros. A concrete candidate sometimes proposed is a Schrödinger-type operator
\[
H=-\frac{d^2}{dx^2}+V(x)
\]
on \(L^2(\mathbb{R}_+)\) (or a suitable half-line realization) whose potential \(V\) is recovered from the explicit formula via the Fourier transform of \(\frac{\Xi'}{\Xi}\).

Assume for contradiction that such a \(V\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}\) exists and that the associated quadratic form
\[
q(f)=\int_0^\infty\bigl(|f'|^2+V|f|^2\bigr)\,dx
\]
is essentially self-adjoint on \(C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)\) with discrete spectrum \(\{t_n\}\). The Weyl asymptotic for the eigenvalue counting function then forces
\[
N(T)=\#\{t_n\le T\}\sim\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi e}+O(\log T).
\]
By the classical Riemann–von Mangoldt formula this is consistent with the known zero-counting asymptotic, so no immediate contradiction arises at the level of density.

The obstruction appears at the level of the local spacing. The pair correlation conjecture (Montgomery) asserts that
\[
\sum_{0<t_n,t_m\le T}f\bigl(\tfrac{t_n-t_m}{2\pi}\log\tfrac{T}{2\pi}\bigr)
\sim\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(u)\Bigl(1-\Bigl(\frac{\sin\pi u}{\pi u}\Bigr)^2\Bigr)\,du
\]
for suitable test functions \(f\). For a Schrödinger operator with smooth potential the local spectral statistics are those of a Poisson point process (or, after unfolding, of GOE only after introducing a random matrix ensemble of potentials). In particular the form factor
\[
\frac1N\sum_{j,k=1}^N e^{i\lambda(E_j-E_k)}
\]
tends to 1 for \(\lambda\) fixed and \(N\to\infty\), contradicting the sine-kernel repulsion term \(1-(\operatorname{sinc})^2\) which is strictly less than 1 near the origin.

Thus no deterministic potential \(V\) of Schrödinger type can reproduce the pair correlation forced by the zeros (assuming RH and the Montgomery conjecture, both of which are weaker than a full spectral realization). Any genuine Hilbert–Pólya operator must therefore incorporate an additional arithmetic or adelic structure (e.g., a non-commutative geometry à la Connes, or an operator on a space of automorphic forms) that is invisible to ordinary one-dimensional quantum mechanics. This rules out a large class of “elementary” spectral approaches while leaving open the possibility of more elaborate realizations.

The argument is conditional on pair correlation and does not produce an unconditional zero-free region or a new equivalence; it merely exhibits a concrete obstruction.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Only an obstruction to naive Schrödinger realizations; no new unconditional progress on RH.
