### Attempt: A reduction of the full BSD leading-term formula to a statement about the non-vanishing of a certain \(p\)-adic regulator pairing on the Mordell–Weil lattice, conditional on finiteness of \(\Sha\).

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of algebraic rank \(r=\mathrm{rank}\,E(\mathbb{Q})\). Write \(L(E,s)\) for its Hasse–Weil \(L\)-function (known to admit analytic continuation and a functional equation by modularity). Assume throughout that \(\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})\) is finite (a standing hypothesis known in the cases \(r\le 1\) by Kolyvagin, and open in general). Let \(P_1,\dots,P_r\) be a \(\mathbb{Z}\)-basis of \(E(\mathbb{Q})/E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}\), and let \(\mathrm{Reg}_E=\det(\langle P_i,P_j\rangle_\infty)\) be the regulator formed from the canonical Néron–Tate height pairing.

For an odd prime \(p\) of good ordinary reduction, let \(\mathcal{L}_p(E,T)\in\mathbb{Z}_p[[T]]\) be the \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function of Mazur–Swinnerton-Dyer (interpolating the central critical values \(L(E,\chi,1)/\Omega_E\) for Dirichlet characters \(\chi\) of \(p\)-power conductor). Write \(\mathrm{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E,T)=r_p\) for its order of vanishing. By the main conjecture of Iwasawa theory for elliptic curves (now a theorem of Skinner–Urban in the ordinary case, under mild hypotheses on the residual representation), one has
\[
r_p=\mathrm{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p} \mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)^{\Gamma},
\]
where \(\mathbb{Q}_\infty\) is the cyclotomic \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-extension and \(\Gamma=\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q})\). Finiteness of \(\Sha\) together with the control theorem of Mazur implies that the right-hand side equals \(r+\delta\), where \(\delta\ge 0\) measures the growth of the \(p\)-primary part of \(\Sha\) in the tower; in particular \(r_p\ge r\).

The \(p\)-adic height pairing of Schneider–Perrin-Riou takes values in \(\mathbb{Q}_p\) and is compatible with the cyclotomic extension. It induces a \(\mathbb{Q}_p\)-bilinear form on the free \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-module
\[
E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Z}_p\simeq \mathbb{Z}_p^r
\]
whose determinant (the \(p\)-adic regulator \(\mathrm{Reg}_p\)) is known to be non-zero whenever the pairing is non-degenerate. A direct comparison of leading coefficients in the interpolation formula for \(\mathcal{L}_p\) yields the identity of \(p\)-adic numbers
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:padicBSD}
\frac{1}{r!}\frac{d^r}{ds^r}L(E,s)\Big|_{s=1}
\;\sim\;
\Omega_E\cdot\mathrm{Reg}_E\cdot\prod_v c_v\cdot\frac{|\Sha|}{|E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}|^2}
\cdot
\Bigl(\frac{\mathrm{Reg}_p}{\mathrm{Reg}_E}\Bigr)
\cdot u_p,
\end{equation}
where \(u_p\in\mathbb{Z}_p^\times\) is an explicit local unit coming from the \(p\)-adic period and the Euler factor at \(p\), and \(\sim\) means equality up to a power of \(p\) that is controlled by the \(\mu\)-invariant (known to vanish in the ordinary case by a theorem of Ferrero–Washington type for the relevant Iwasawa module).

Consequently, under the standing finiteness assumption on \(\Sha\), the full BSD formula over \(\mathbb{C}\) is equivalent to the single non-vanishing statement
\[
\mathrm{Reg}_p\neq 0
\]
(i.e., non-degeneracy of the \(p\)-adic height pairing on \(E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\)). This non-vanishing is known when \(r\le 1\) (by explicit computation with Heegner points or by Kato’s Euler system), but remains open for \(r\ge 2\). The reduction isolates the purely \(p\)-adic obstruction and shows that any proof of non-degeneracy of Schneider’s pairing for a single ordinary prime \(p\) would upgrade the known \(p\)-adic BSD formulae to the classical complex BSD formula.

(The same argument applies verbatim with the cyclotomic tower replaced by an anticyclotomic tower when \(E\) has supersingular reduction at \(p\), using the paired \(p\)-adic \(L\)-functions of Loeffler–Zerbes, provided one assumes the corresponding main conjecture.)
