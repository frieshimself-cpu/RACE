### Attempt: Conditional a priori bound via controlled vortex stretching alignment

Consider the incompressible Navier–Stokes system on \(\mathbb{R}^3\times[0,T)\)
\[
\partial_t u+(u\cdot\nabla)u=\nu\Delta u-\nabla p,\qquad\operatorname{div}u=0,
\]
with smooth, divergence-free initial data \(u_0\in H^s\) (\(s\ge3\)) of finite energy. Let \(\omega=\nabla\times u\) and write the vorticity formulation
\[
\partial_t\omega+(u\cdot\nabla)\omega=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u+\nu\Delta\omega.
\]
The deformation tensor \(S=\frac12(\nabla u+(\nabla u)^\top)\) satisfies \(S\omega=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u\). In Fourier space the Calderón–Zygmund relation
\[
\widehat{S}_{ij}(\xi)= \Bigl(\frac{\xi_i\xi_k}{|\xi|^2}\delta_{jl}+\frac{\xi_j\xi_l}{|\xi|^2}\delta_{ik}-\frac12\delta_{ij}\frac{\xi_k\xi_l}{|\xi|^2}\Bigr)\widehat{\omega}_k
\]
holds (up to the usual projection onto divergence-free fields). Consequently the pointwise stretching rate is completely determined by the Riesz transforms of \(\omega\).

Define the alignment cosine
\[
\cos\theta(x,t)=\frac{\omega\cdot S\omega}{|\omega|\,|S\omega|}\qquad\bigl(|\omega|\ne0\bigr).
\]
A direct energy estimate on the enstrophy \(\mathcal{E}(t)=\frac12\|\omega(t)\|_{L^2}^2\) yields
\[
\frac{d\mathcal{E}}{dt}+\nu\|\nabla\omega\|_{L^2}^2=\int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\omega\cdot S\omega\,dx=\int|\omega|^2|S|\cos\theta\,dx.
\]
By the Calderón–Zygmund \(L^p\)-boundedness of \(S\) and Hölder,
\[
\biggl|\int|\omega|^2|S|\cos\theta\,dx\biggr|\le C\|\omega\|_{L^3}^2\|S\|_{L^3}\le C\|\omega\|_{L^3}^3.
\]
If one assumes the geometric control
\begin{equation}\label{align}
|\cos\theta(x,t)|\le\frac{c_*}{1+\log(e+|\omega(x,t)|)}\qquad\text{a.e.\ on }\{|\omega|\ge1\}
\end{equation}
for a sufficiently small absolute constant \(c_*>0\), the cubic term is absorbed by the dissipative integral after a standard logarithmic Sobolev interpolation:
\[
\|\omega\|_{L^3}^3\le\varepsilon\|\nabla\omega\|_{L^2}^2+C_\varepsilon\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2\bigl(1+\log(e+\|\omega\|_{L^\infty})\bigr).
\]
A further application of the Beale–Kato–Majda inequality then produces the closed differential inequality
\[
\frac{d\mathcal{E}}{dt}\le C\mathcal{E}\bigl(1+\log(e+\mathcal{E})\bigr),
\]
which integrates to a double-exponential bound on \([0,T)\) and therefore prevents \(\|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}\) from blowing up in finite time. Smoothness up to time \(T\) follows by standard bootstrapping.

The obstruction is that \eqref{align} is an extra structural assumption not implied by the NS dynamics alone. While numerical evidence and the Constantin–Fefferman geometric criterion suggest that regions of high strain tend to anti-align with vorticity, no a-priori mechanism forces the cosine to decay at the precise logarithmic rate required to close the estimate for arbitrary smooth finite-energy data. Thus the argument yields only a conditional regularity statement: any putative singularity must violate the alignment bound \eqref{align} on a set of positive measure.

VERDICT: PARTIAL
REASON: Conditional regularity under log-alignment of vorticity; no unconditional control of the cosine.
