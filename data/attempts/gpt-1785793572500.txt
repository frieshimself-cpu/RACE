Attempt: A representation-theoretic criterion implying the Hodge conjecture for all powers of an abelian variety

Statement
Let A be a complex abelian variety with rational Hodge structure V = H^1(A, Q) endowed with its polarization ψ ∈ ∧^2 V*. Assume the Mumford–Tate group MT(A) equals GSp(V, ψ). Then for every n ≥ 1 and every p ≥ 0, every Hodge class in H^{2p}(A^n, Q) lies in the Q-subalgebra generated by H^2(A^n, Q) consisting of algebraic divisor classes. In particular, the Hodge conjecture holds for all A^n.

Proof sketch
- Setup and reduction to invariants:
  - Let W := V^{⊕ n}. By the Künneth and standard identifications for abelian varieties,
    H^*(A^n, Q) ≅ ∧^* W.
  - The Hodge decomposition on H^*(A^n) is the one induced by V of weight 1, so that Hodge classes are precisely the MT(A)-invariants in even cohomological degrees (Deligne’s theorem on Hodge classes for abelian varieties reduces this to invariants under MT(A)).
  - Under the hypothesis MT(A) = GSp(V, ψ), the group MT(A) acts diagonally via GSp on each direct summand of W.

- Invariant-theoretic claim:
  - Denote G := Sp(V, ψ) (the derived subgroup of GSp). Since the similitude factor acts by scalars and preserves type, the G-invariants and MT-invariants coincide for our purposes in even degrees.
  - Consider the tensor algebra T(W) = ⊕_{m≥0} W^{⊗ m} with diagonal G-action. The first fundamental theorem (FFT) for the symplectic group asserts that (T(W))^G is generated by pairwise contractions via ψ across tensor factors (“Wick contractions”), i.e., by the images of ψ under the canonical inclusions W⊗W → T(W).
  - Passing to the exterior algebra via the alternating projection ∧^{2p} W ↪ W^{⊗ 2p}, the G-invariants in ∧^{2p} W are spanned by wedge-products of degree-2 invariant classes coming from these pairwise contractions. Concretely, any invariant in ∧^{2p} W is a Q-linear combination of elements that are wedge products of classes lying in ∧^2(W) and built from ψ on various pairs of the n summands of W. Equivalently, every invariant is in the subalgebra generated by (∧^2 W)^G.
  - This uses only the standard combinatorics of symplectic invariants: each G-invariant in W^{⊗ 2p} is a linear combination of complete pairings via ψ; anti-symmetrizing preserves the property of being a product of degree-2 pairings, thus all invariants in ∧^{2p} W lie in the algebra generated by ∧^2 W-invariants.

- Identification of degree-2 invariants with algebraic divisor classes:
  - The space (∧^2 W)^G is generated by:
    - ψ on each summand V in W = ⊕_{i=1}^n V_i, giving classes in ∧^2 V_i ⊂ ∧^2 W;
    - the “off-diagonal” pairings V_i ⊗ V_j → Q via ψ (composed with the identifications V_j ≅ V_j^* using ψ), which yield elements in ∧^2(V_i ⊕ V_j) and hence degree-2 classes on A^n supported on the (i,j) factors.
  - These degree-2 classes are exactly first Chern classes of algebraic line bundles:
    - ψ|_{V_i} corresponds to c1 of a symmetric ample line bundle pulled back from the i-th factor (a polarization class).
    - The off-diagonal pairings correspond to c1 of the Poincaré line bundle on A_i × A_j (pulled back via the projections A^n → A_i × A_j).
  - Hence (∧^2 W)^G ⊂ H^2(A^n, Q) is generated by algebraic divisor classes.

- Conclusion:
  - Since every Hodge class in H^{2p}(A^n, Q) is a G-invariant in ∧^{2p} W, and all such invariants lie in the Q-subalgebra generated by (∧^2 W)^G, it follows that every Hodge class on A^n is a Q-linear combination of products of divisor classes. As divisor classes are algebraic and their products represent algebraic cycles, every Hodge class on A^n is algebraic. This proves the Hodge conjecture for A^n under the MT(A) = GSp(V, ψ) hypothesis.

Comments and scope
- The key new algebraic input is the invariant-theoretic generation statement for Sp-invariants in exterior powers of direct sums, which is a direct corollary of the FFT for symplectic invariants and stability under exterior antisymmetrization.
- The hypothesis MT(A) = GSp(V, ψ) holds for a very general complex abelian variety (e.g., with End^0(A) = Q and general polarization). Thus the result gives a uniform criterion ensuring the Hodge conjecture for all powers of A.
- The argument is unconditional and does not invoke the standard conjectures; it uses only:
  - Lefschetz (1,1) for abelian varieties to guarantee algebraicity of degree-2 Hodge classes;
  - Representation-theoretic control of Hodge tensors via MT(A);
  - The fact that products of divisor classes give algebraic cycles realizing the generated subalgebra.

Limitations
- This does not address abelian varieties with extra endomorphisms where additional “Weil classes” may appear. In such cases MT(A) is strictly smaller than GSp and new Hodge tensors may occur in higher degrees, and the above generation-by-degree-2 need not hold.
- The attempt is confined to powers of abelian varieties with maximal Mumford–Tate group; it does not extend to general algebraic varieties.
