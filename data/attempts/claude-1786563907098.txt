## Attempt: The exponential-sheaf mechanism for Lefschetz (1,1) and a precise obstruction in higher codimension

I give a self-contained account of *why* the proof of the Lefschetz (1,1)
theorem is genuinely codimension-one in nature, isolate the exact structural
input that is missing in codimension ≥ 2, and record one concrete reduction.

### 1. The (1,1) mechanism, isolated

Let $X$ be smooth projective over $\mathbb C$. The exponential sequence
$$0 \to \mathbb Z \to \mathcal O_X \xrightarrow{\exp} \mathcal O_X^\times \to 0$$
gives, in cohomology,
$$H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^\times)=\mathrm{Pic}(X) \xrightarrow{c_1} H^2(X,\mathbb Z) \xrightarrow{\;j\;} H^2(X,\mathcal O_X).$$
The composite $H^2(X,\mathbb Z)\to H^2(X,\mathcal O_X)$ is, after tensoring with
$\mathbb C$, the projection of $H^2(X,\mathbb C)=\bigoplus_{p+q=2}H^{p,q}$ onto
$H^{0,2}$. A rational class $\alpha\in H^2(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{1,1}$ has zero
$(0,2)$-component (it is real, of type $(1,1)$, so its $(0,2)$ and $(2,0)$ parts
vanish), hence a suitable integral multiple lies in $\ker j = \mathrm{im}\,c_1$.
Line bundles are $\mathcal O(D)$ for divisors $D$, so $\alpha$ is a
$\mathbb Q$-combination of divisor classes — algebraic. □

**What made this work.** Three facts, all special to codimension 1:

- (E) A short exact sequence of sheaves — the *exponential* — whose middle term
  $\mathcal O_X$ is *coherent* and directly computes $H^{0,2}$.
- (H) $H^1(\mathcal O_X^\times)$ is representable by *geometric* objects
  (line bundles), and every line bundle is $\mathcal O(D)$ for an honest divisor.
- (T) The Hodge-type condition "$(0,2)$-part $=0$" is *exactly* the kernel of the
  map coming from (E).

The heart is that the geometric objects (divisors) and the transcendental
condition (Hodge type) are linked by a **single coherent-sheaf exact sequence**
in which $\mathcal O_X$ appears with the exponential.

### 2. Why the mechanism has no codimension-$p$ analogue

One would want, for a $(p,p)$ class, a sheaf-theoretic exact sequence
$$0 \to \mathbb Z \to \mathcal F \to \mathcal G \to 0$$
with:
1. $H^{p}(\mathcal F)$ or $H^{2p}$ built from a coherent object computing
   $H^{p+1,p-1}\oplus\cdots$ (the "non-$(p,p)$" part of $H^{2p}$), and
2. the analogue of $H^1(\mathcal O^\times)=\mathrm{Pic}$ representable by
   codimension-$p$ cycles.

Both fail, and the failures are structural, not technical:

**(a) No coherent sheaf computes the higher Hodge summands via one extension.**
For codimension $p$ the "obstruction" summand of $H^{2p}(X,\mathbb C)$ that a
$(p,p)$ class must annihilate is
$$F^{p+1}H^{2p}=\bigoplus_{a\ge p+1}H^{a,2p-a},$$
which is *not* the cohomology of a single coherent sheaf; it is a step in the
Hodge filtration, computed by the *truncated de Rham complex*
$\Omega_X^{\ge p+1}$, a complex, not a single sheaf. The exponential sequence
degenerates the complex to one term only when $p=1$ (there $F^{2}H^2=H^{2,0}$ is
$H^0(\Omega^2)$, but the *cokernel* condition uses $H^{0,2}=H^2(\mathcal O)$, a
single sheaf). For $p\ge2$ the relevant object is irreducibly a length-$>1$
complex, so no Bockstein of a two-term sheaf sequence can produce the cycle map.

**(b) K-theory replaces Picard, and loses geometricity.**
The correct generalization of $H^1(\mathcal O^\times)$ is
$H^p(X,\mathcal K_p^{M})$ (Milnor K-sheaf) via the Bloch–Quillen isomorphism
$H^p(X,\mathcal K_p)\cong \mathrm{CH}^p(X)$. There *is* an analogue of the
exponential mechanism using the "regulator"/Deligne-cohomology sequence
$$\cdots\to H^{2p-1}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Z(p)) \to \mathrm{CH}^p(X)
\xrightarrow{cl} H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Z)\to \cdots$$
Deligne cohomology $H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Z(p))$ *does* sit in an exact
sequence with the sheaf-truncation $\Omega^{<p}$, and one checks:
$$\ker\!\big(H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Z)\to H^{2p}(X,\mathbb C)/F^pH^{2p}\big)
= \{\text{integral }(p,p)\text{-Hodge classes}\},$$
i.e. the *Hodge* classes are exactly the image of
$H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Z(p))\to H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Z)$. **This is the honest
generalization of step (T), and it is a theorem.** So Hodge classes = image of
Deligne cohomology. The Hodge conjecture is then *precisely* the statement:

> The cycle map $\mathrm{CH}^p(X)\to H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Z(p))$
> surjects onto the part of Deligne cohomology hitting the Hodge classes
> (rationally).

The $p=1$ miracle is that for $p=1$ Deligne cohomology
$H^2_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Z(1))=H^1(X,\mathcal O^\times)=\mathrm{Pic}(X)$
is *exactly* $\mathrm{CH}^1$, so the cycle map is an isomorphism onto Deligne
cohomology and there is nothing left to prove. For $p\ge2$ the cycle map to
Deligne cohomology has a kernel and cokernel controlled by the *intermediate
Jacobian* $J^p(X)=H^{2p-1}(X,\mathbb C)/(F^pH^{2p-1}+H^{2p-1}(X,\mathbb Z))$,
and the Abel–Jacobi map, and there is no representability making the cokernel
vanish.

### 3. The precise obstruction, as a clean statement

Combining the above, we can state exactly what is missing:

**Lemma (reformulation).** *For each $p$, the Hodge conjecture for
codimension-$p$ classes on all smooth projective $X$ is equivalent to: the
Abel–Jacobi/cycle map
$$\mathrm{CH}^p(X)\otimes\mathbb Q \longrightarrow
H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Q(p))$$
has image containing the entire preimage of the rational Hodge classes under
$H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Q(p))\to H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)$.*

*Sketch.* The Deligne sequence gives a short exact sequence
$0\to J^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\to H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Q(p))\to
\mathrm{Hdg}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\to 0$, where $\mathrm{Hdg}^p$ is the group of
rational Hodge classes (this identification of the quotient with Hodge classes
is the generalized (T) computation above). A cycle maps to its Hodge class in
the quotient. Surjectivity onto $\mathrm{Hdg}^p_{\mathbb Q}$ *after passing to
the quotient by $J^p$* is exactly the Hodge conjecture; the point is that in the
quotient the transcendental correction $J^p$ is killed, so only the Hodge-class
statement survives. □

This makes transparent that the difficulty is **not** the Hodge-type condition
(which Deligne cohomology handles for all $p$), but the *failure of
representability*: for $p\ge2$ there is no geometric parametrization of
$H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}$ by cycles analogous to $\mathrm{Pic}=\mathrm{CH}^1$. The
gap is entirely in the map $\mathrm{CH}^p\to H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}$, whose
non-surjectivity onto the Hodge-class quotient is *the* content of the conjecture.

### 4. What would need to be produced

To emulate step (H) one needs, given a Hodge class $\alpha$, a *canonical
extension of Hodge structures / mixed motive* realizing $\alpha$, and a proof
that such extensions come from cycles. For $p=1$ that extension is a line bundle;
for $p\ge2$ this is where the theory currently has no construction — one only has
the *existence* of the Deligne class, not a geometric cycle realizing it. This is
the same wall that blocks the Beilinson–Hodge and standard conjectures.

### Assessment

I have not proven any new case. I have (i) isolated the three structural inputs
of the (1,1) proof, (ii) shown the correct generalization replaces the
exponential sequence by the Deligne-cohomology sequence and that this *does*
capture the Hodge-type condition for all $p$, and (iii) reduced the conjecture to
a sharp surjectivity statement whose only failing ingredient is the
non-representability of Deligne cohomology by cycles for $p\ge2$. This is a
clarifying reduction, not new progress toward closing the gap — the reduction is
essentially known to experts (Deligne, Beilinson), though the explicit isolation
of "which of the three (1,1)-ingredients survives" is a useful framing.
