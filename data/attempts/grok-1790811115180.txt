### Attempt: Obstruction to a direct continuum limit from strong-coupling mass gap via Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction

We work throughout with compact simple \(G=\mathrm{SU}(N)\), \(N\ge 2\). Let \(\Lambda_a\subset\mathbb{R}^4\) be a finite toroidal lattice of spacing \(a>0\) and side length \(L=Na\) with \(N\) even. The Wilson action
\[
S_a(U)=\frac{1}{g_0^2}\sum_{p\subset\Lambda_a}\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(1-U_p)
\]
defines a probability measure \(\mathrm{d}\mu_a\) on the compact space of \(G\)-valued link variables. Reflection positivity through hyperplanes midway between lattice sites yields a positive-semidefinite transfer matrix \(T_a\) on the Hilbert space \(\mathcal{H}_a\) of \(G\)-invariant functions of the spatial links; the mass gap of the lattice theory is
\[
m(a):=-\frac1a\log\bigl(\|T_a|_{\mathbf{1}^\perp}\|\bigr)\in(0,\infty].
\]
It is classical (strong-coupling cluster expansion) that there exist \(g_*<\infty\) and \(c,C>0\) such that
\[
m(a)\ge\frac{c}{a}\exp\bigl(-C/g_0^2\bigr)\qquad\text{for all }g_0\ge g_*.
\]
The continuum Yang–Mills measure, if it exists, is expected to arise as a weak limit of the finite-volume measures after renormalization \(g_0=g_0(a)\) chosen so that a physical scale (e.g., the string tension \(\sigma(a)\)) remains fixed while \(a\to0\). Asymptotic freedom supplies the candidate running
\[
\frac1{g_0^2(a)}=\frac{11N}{24\pi^2}\log\bigl(\frac1{a\Lambda}\bigr)+O(1),
\]
with \(\Lambda>0\) a renormalization-group invariant. Substituting this running into the strong-coupling lower bound immediately yields
\[
m(a)\ge\frac{c}{a}\exp\bigl(-C'\log(1/(a\Lambda))\bigr)=\frac{c}{a}(a\Lambda)^{C'}.
\]
For any \(C'>1\) the right-hand side tends to \(0\) as \(a\to0\). Thus the only information the strong-coupling expansion can give is that the continuum mass gap, if positive, must be generated by non-perturbative effects that become visible only after the continuum limit has already been taken; the lattice gap itself vanishes in physical units under the asymptotically free running.

A more precise obstruction appears when one attempts to pass to the continuum via the Osterwalder–Schrader axioms. Suppose a sequence \(a_n\to0\) and renormalized couplings \(g_0(a_n)\) exist such that the finite-volume Schwinger functions of gauge-invariant local operators (Wilson loops, Polyakov lines, etc.) converge to tempered distributions \(S_n\) on \(\mathbb{R}^{4n}\) that satisfy OS positivity, Euclidean invariance, and clustering. The reconstructed Minkowski theory then possesses a mass gap if and only if the truncated two-point function of the lowest-dimensional gauge-invariant scalar decays exponentially. On the lattice the corresponding truncated correlator admits the spectral representation
\[
\langle\mathcal{O}(x)\mathcal{O}(0)\rangle_a^T=\int_{m(a)}^\infty\mathrm{d}\rho_a(\lambda)\,e^{-\lambda|x_0|},
\]
where \(\operatorname{supp}\rho_a\subset[m(a),\infty)\). Under the running above one has \(m(a_n)\to0\), so the only way the continuum two-point function can retain a gap is if the spectral measures \(\rho_{a_n}\) develop a gap that opens at a rate exactly compensating the vanishing of \(m(a_n)\). No a-priori bound preventing the accumulation of eigenvalues at zero is known; the cluster expansion that produces the lattice gap ceases to be uniform once \(g_0(a)\) enters the weak-coupling regime. Consequently the existence of a strictly positive continuum mass gap cannot be read off from any uniform lattice estimate and requires a new, genuinely continuum mechanism (e.g., a uniform infrared bound on the renormalized glueball propagator that survives the limit \(a\to0\)).

This shows that every strategy which first establishes a mass gap at fixed \(a>0\) and only afterwards removes the cutoff is blocked by asymptotic freedom: the gap evaporates in physical units before the continuum is reached. Any successful proof must therefore control the infrared behaviour simultaneously with the ultraviolet limit.

VERDICT: FAILED
REASON: Strong-coupling gap evaporates under AF running; no continuum IR bound obtained.
