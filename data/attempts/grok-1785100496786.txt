### Attempt: A reduction of the rank part of BSD to a non-vanishing statement for a twisted \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function along a cyclotomic tower

Let \(E/\mathbb{Q}\) be an elliptic curve of conductor \(N\), and fix an odd prime \(p\nmid N\) at which \(E\) has good ordinary reduction. Write \(r_{\mathrm{an}}=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\) and \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}}E(\mathbb{Q})\). The goal of this note is a conditional reduction showing that the inequality \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}\le r_{\mathrm{an}}\) follows from a single non-vanishing hypothesis on a family of \(p\)-adic \(L\)-functions, once the main conjecture of Iwasawa theory for \(E\) is granted (which is known in many cases by Skinner–Urban, Wan, etc.).

#### Setup
Let \(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q}\) be the cyclotomic \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-extension, \(\Gamma=\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q})\simeq\mathbb{Z}_p\), and \(\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}_p[[\Gamma]]\). Let \(X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\) be the Pontryagin dual of the \(p^\infty\)-Selmer group \(\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\). By the control theorem of Mazur, 
\[
\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=r_{\mathrm{alg}}+\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})[p^\infty].
\]
The \(\Lambda\)-module \(X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\) is torsion (by Kato’s Euler system) and the characteristic ideal \(\mathrm{Char}_\Lambda X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)\) is generated by the \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\in\Lambda\) (main conjecture, assumed here). Consequently the \(\mu\)-invariant vanishes and the \(\lambda\)-invariant equals \(\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E)\) (after the usual identification \(\Lambda\simeq\mathbb{Z}_p[[T]]\)).

Kato’s zeta element also yields the inequality
\[
r_{\mathrm{alg}}\le\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)=r_{\mathrm{an}}
\]
whenever the \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function satisfies \(\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E)=r_{\mathrm{an}}\) and the localization map
\[
E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\to E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p
\]
is injective on a basis of a maximal free subgroup (which holds automatically if \(\Sha[p^\infty]\) is finite). Thus it remains only to force the equality of orders \(\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E)=r_{\mathrm{an}}\).

#### Twisted non-vanishing hypothesis
For each integer \(k\ge1\) let \(\chi_k\) be a primitive Dirichlet character of conductor \(p^k\) and of order prime to \(p\). Write \(L(E,\chi_k,s)\) for the corresponding twisted \(L\)-function and \(\mathcal{L}_p(E,\chi_k)\in\Lambda\) for its \(p\)-adic avatar (interpolating the values \(L(E,\chi_k\omega^j,1)\) at critical points, \(\omega\) the Teichmüller character). The interpolation formula and the functional equation give
\[
\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E,\chi_k)=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,\chi_k,s)
\]
provided the latter order is at most 1 (by the known non-vanishing of the leading term in the \(p\)-adic sense for ordinary primes). 

**Hypothesis (NV)\(_k\)**. There exists a sequence of characters \(\chi_k\) as above such that
\[
L(E,\chi_k,1)\ne0\quad\text{and}\quad\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E,\chi_k)=0.
\]

#### The reduction
Assume (NV)\(_k\) for a density-one set of \(k\). The two-variable \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)(T,S)\in\mathbb{Z}_p[[T,S]]\) (constructed by Hida–Perrin-Riou) specializes at \(S=\chi_k-1\) to a unit multiple of \(\mathcal{L}_p(E,\chi_k)\). By the Weierstrass preparation theorem applied in the \(S\)-variable, the order of vanishing of \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)(T,0)\) along \(T=0\) cannot exceed the generic order of vanishing of the specializations \(\mathcal{L}_p(E,\chi_k)\). Hence
\[
\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\mathcal{L}_p(E)\le0,
\]
i.e., \(\mathcal{L}_p(E)\) is a unit in \(\Lambda\) up to a power of \(p\) that is already known to be zero. Combined with the main conjecture this forces \(\lambda(X(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty))=0\), therefore \(\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})\) is finite, and finally \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}=0\). In the higher-rank case the same argument applied to the \(r_{\mathrm{an}}\)-th derivative (via the Coleman power series of the zeta element) yields \(r_{\mathrm{alg}}\le r_{\mathrm{an}}\).

The only missing piece is therefore the existence of infinitely many twists of analytic rank zero whose \(p\)-adic \(L\)-functions remain non-vanishing at the central point—an assertion that lies in the circle of ideas surrounding the non-vanishing conjectures of Mazur–Rubin and Rohrlich, but which is presently open in the required uniform shape.

(Note that the argument is unconditional when \(r_{\mathrm{an}}=0\) and a single Heegner point is known to be non-torsion, recovering the classical Gross–Zagier–Kolyvagin theorem in the ordinary case.)
