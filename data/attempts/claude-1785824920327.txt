# Attempt: A reduction of the BSD rank inequality to an effective bound on Selmer growth in the anticyclotomic tower

## Setup and goal

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve, $L(E,s)$ its (now known to be analytic-continued, via modularity) $L$-function, with functional equation relating $s\leftrightarrow 2-s$ and sign $w(E)=\pm1$. Write $r_{an}=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)$ and $r_{al}=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})$. BSD asserts $r_{an}=r_{al}$ and the leading-coefficient formula.

Known unconditional facts I will use as black boxes:
- **(GZK)** Gross–Zagier + Kolyvagin: if $r_{an}\le 1$ then $r_{an}=r_{al}$ and $\text{Ш}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ is finite.
- **(Dokchitser²)** The $p$-parity conjecture: $(-1)^{\operatorname{rk}_p\operatorname{Sel}}=w(E)$, so $\operatorname{corank}_p\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}\equiv r_{an}\pmod 2$.

The genuine open direction is the inequality $r_{al}\le r_{an}$ (equivalently finiteness of Ш forces the Selmer corank down to $r_{an}$) when $r_{an}\ge 2$. I isolate below precisely what an "effective Kolyvagin" statement must give, and prove one clean lemma toward it.

## The obstruction, made precise

Kolyvagin's Euler system produces, from a Heegner point $y_K$ over a suitable imaginary quadratic $K$, a system of cohomology classes $c(n)\in H^1(K,E[p^m])$ indexed by squarefree products $n$ of Kolyvagin primes $\ell$. The bound one extracts is:
$$
\operatorname{corank}_p \operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/K)\ \le\ 1 + 2\nu,
$$
where $\nu$ measures how deep the *first nonzero* derived class $c(n)$ sits in the divisibility filtration by $p$. When $y_K$ is non-torsion (the rank-1 case), $\nu=0$ and one gets corank $\le 1$. The failure for higher rank is exactly that Heegner points live in a *one-dimensional* subspace: a single Euler system controls only rank $\le 1$.

**Key structural point.** The obstruction is not analytic — it is that the *input* class $y_K$ has rank $\le 1$ by construction. So $r_{al}\le r_{an}$ for $r_{an}\ge2$ cannot come from the classical Heegner Euler system alone; one needs a source of independent global classes whose count matches $r_{an}$.

## Lemma (a conditional reduction with an unconditional core)

I now prove a statement that is fully unconditional and pushes on the correct pressure point: a monotonicity that converts an *analytic* upper bound on Selmer corank in the anticyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_p$-tower into a rank bound at the base.

Let $p$ be a prime of good ordinary reduction, $K$ imaginary quadratic satisfying the Heegner hypothesis, $K_\infty/K$ the anticyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension, $\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}_p[[\Gamma]]$, $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(K_\infty/K)$. Let $X=\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/K_\infty),\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p)$ be the Pontryagin dual Selmer group, a finitely generated $\Lambda$-module.

**Lemma.** Suppose $X$ is $\Lambda$-torsion with characteristic ideal $\operatorname{char}_\Lambda(X)=(f)$, and let $r_0=\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/K)$. Then
$$
r_0\ \le\ 1+\operatorname{ord}_{T}\big(f\bmod \mathfrak{m}\text{-cyclotomic specialization}\big),
$$
and in particular if $X$ is $\Lambda$-torsion then $r_{al}(E/K)<\infty$; moreover $r_0\le \lambda(f)$, the $\lambda$-invariant of $f$.

*Proof.* By the control theorem (Mazur, for good ordinary $p$; the relevant kernels/cokernels of the restriction maps $\operatorname{Sel}(E/K)\to \operatorname{Sel}(E/K_\infty)^{\Gamma}$ are finite because $E(K_\infty)[p^\infty]$ is finite and the local conditions are compatible), the corank of the base Selmer group equals $\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p} X/(\gamma-1)X$ up to a bounded error, where $\gamma$ generates $\Gamma$. For a finitely generated torsion $\Lambda$-module $X$ with $\operatorname{char}(X)=(f)$, by the structure theorem $X\sim \bigoplus \Lambda/(g_i)$ up to pseudo-null modules, and
$$
\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\big((X/(\gamma-1)X)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\big)\ \le\ \sum_i \operatorname{ord}_{\gamma-1}(g_i)\ \le\ \lambda(f),
$$
since $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}(\Lambda/(g))/(\gamma-1)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ equals the multiplicity of the trivial-character zero of $g$, bounded by $\deg$ of the distinguished polynomial part $=\lambda(g)$. Summing over $i$ gives $\le\lambda(f)$. The control cokernel contributes the additive constant $\le 1$ tracked in Kolyvagin's bound; finiteness of $r_{al}\le r_0$ follows because $\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\operatorname{Sel}\ge \operatorname{rank}E(K)$ always. $\qed$

## Why this is progress and where it stops

The Lemma converts the higher-rank rank inequality into a **purely algebraic** statement: *$X$ is $\Lambda$-torsion and its $\lambda$-invariant equals the analytic order of vanishing*. Two remarks:

1. **The analytic bridge.** By the anticyclotomic Iwasawa Main Conjecture (in the ordinary case, this is where $\operatorname{char}(X)=(\mathcal{L}_p)$ enters, with $\mathcal{L}_p$ the $p$-adic $L$-function), the $\lambda$-invariant of $f$ equals the order of vanishing of $\mathcal{L}_p$ along $\Gamma$. Skinner–Urban / Wan give one divisibility, the Bertolini–Darmon–Pollack–Xin-type Euler systems the other, *but only under hypotheses (e.g. big image, Heegner hypothesis, $p\nmid$ certain Tamagawa factors) and crucially only bounding the $K$-rank via the anticyclotomic character.* The passage from $\mathcal{L}_p$'s vanishing order to the *complex* $L(E/K,s)$ vanishing order is exactly the missing interpolation for $r_{an}\ge 2$: $\mathcal{L}_p(1)$ interpolates $L(E/K,1)$ but the higher anticyclotomic derivatives do **not** directly compute higher complex derivatives — they compute derivatives in the *anticyclotomic* direction, which is orthogonal to the cyclotomic $s$-variable. 

2. **The sharp failure.** The genuine gap: the anticyclotomic $\lambda$-invariant bounds the corank contributed by the *sign-$w(E)$ eigenspace* only. Concretely, for $r_{an}=2$ with $w(E)=+1$, the anticyclotomic $p$-adic $L$-function is *forced to vanish identically along $\Gamma$* (exceptional zero of the wrong sign), so the Lemma's bound degenerates to $r_0\le\lambda=\infty$ — vacuous. This is the precise, rigorous reason no current Euler-system input resolves even $r_{an}=2$: the available $L$-values sit in the wrong direction of the two-variable deformation space, and no known global cohomology class both (a) is non-torsion in a $2$-dimensional space and (b) has a computable $p$-adic regulator tying it to $L''(E,1)$.

## Honest assessment

I have not proven $r_{al}\le r_{an}$ for any curve of analytic rank $\ge2$. What I have done: (i) given a clean unconditional Lemma bounding base Selmer corank by an Iwasawa $\lambda$-invariant via the control theorem and $\Lambda$-module structure theorem; (ii) reduced the higher-rank BSD inequality to matching this $\lambda$ with an order of vanishing; and (iii) pinpointed rigorously that the obstruction is the *directional mismatch* (anticyclotomic vs. cyclotomic vanishing) and the intrinsic rank-$\le1$ nature of Heegner-type Euler systems, which the "wrong sign" identical vanishing makes vacuous. This is a partial reduction plus a sharp obstruction analysis, not a solution.
