# Attempt: A quantitative obstruction lemma for BSD in rank ≥ 2

I focus on a specific, self-contained sub-question: *what exactly is missing*, in a
precise analytic form, to extend the known rank ≤ 1 results toward the full
leading-coefficient formula. I isolate the obstruction as a transcendence /
period-regulator comparison and prove a clean conditional reduction.

## Setup

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve of conductor $N$, with modular
parametrization $\phi: X_0(N) \to E$ and Manin constant $c_0$. Write
$L(E,s)$ for its $L$-function, $\Omega_E$ the real period, $\mathrm{Reg}(E)$ the
regulator on $E(\mathbb{Q})/\mathrm{tors}$, $\Sha$ the Tate–Shafarevich group,
$c_p$ the Tamagawa numbers, $T = |E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}|$. Full BSD asserts
$$
\mathcal{B}(E) := \frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!\,\Omega_E\,\mathrm{Reg}(E)}
= \frac{|\Sha|\,\prod_p c_p}{T^2},\qquad r=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q}).
$$

The right side is a **rational number** (indeed the RHS is manifestly in
$\mathbb{Q}$, with $|\Sha|$ conjecturally finite). So a necessary consequence of
BSD is:

**(Rationality) $\mathcal{B}(E)\in\mathbb{Q}$.**

In rank $0$ and $1$ this rationality is a *theorem* (Gross–Zagier + Kolyvagin
give $\mathrm{Reg}$ and $L'$ commensurate via Heegner points; rank $0$ via
modular symbols / Kato). For $r\ge 2$ even the rationality of $\mathcal{B}(E)$ is
open. I make the structure of this gap precise.

## Lemma (Localization of the obstruction)

*Fix $E$ with $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=r\ge 2$. Suppose:*

1. *(Analytic) $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)=r$ (analytic rank = algebraic rank);*
2. *(Period–regulator commensurability) $L^{(r)}(E,1)/(r!\,\Omega_E)\in
   \mathbb{Q}\cdot\mathrm{Reg}(E)$.*

*Then $\mathcal{B}(E)\in\mathbb{Q}$, and consequently the finiteness of $\Sha$
together with the exact value $|\Sha|$ is determined by BSD up to the rational
factor $\mathcal{B}(E)T^2/\prod_p c_p$.*

**Proof.** Immediate from the definitions: (1) guarantees
$L^{(r)}(E,1)\ne 0$ and that the Taylor coefficient at order $r$ is the relevant
leading term. (2) is literally the statement $\mathcal{B}(E)\in\mathbb{Q}$. The
final clause is the rearrangement
$|\Sha| = \mathcal{B}(E)\,T^2/\prod_p c_p$, a product of rationals. $\qquad\blacksquare$

The content is that condition (2) — *not* analyticity — is the true wall for
$r\ge 2$. Below I argue this is genuinely a **transcendence** statement, and that
the standard rank-1 machinery cannot reach it.

## Why (2) is not reducible to rank-1 methods

In rank $1$, Gross–Zagier expresses $L'(E,1)$ as
$$
L'(E,1) = \frac{\|\hat y_K\|^2}{c\cdot \sqrt{|D|}}\cdot(\text{explicit rational}),
$$
where $\hat y_K$ is a Heegner point and $\|\cdot\|$ its Néron–Tate height. Crucially
$\|\hat y_K\|^2 = \mathrm{Reg}(E)\times(\text{rational})$ *because the point
generates a finite-index subgroup of the rank-1 group*: a single height equals the
regulator up to $\mathbb{Q}$.

For $r\ge 2$ the analogue would need a supply of $r$ independent algebraic points
whose height *pairing matrix* has determinant commensurable with
$L^{(r)}(E,1)/(r!\,\Omega_E)$. The Gross–Zagier / Zhang generalizations
(higher weight, Gross–Kohnen–Zagier relations, and derivatives of Rankin–Selberg
$L$-functions) produce points *from Heegner constructions*, but these Heegner-type
points span at most a **rank-1** subspace of $E(K)^-$ for a fixed imaginary
quadratic $K$; over $\mathbb{Q}$ the derived points do not multiply the achievable
rank. Concretely:

**Observation.** If $E(\mathbb{Q})$ has rank $r\ge 2$, then for every imaginary
quadratic $K$ with the Heegner hypothesis, the sign of the functional equation of
$E/K$ is $-1$, forcing $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E/K,s)$ to be *odd*, while
$L(E/K,s)=L(E,s)L(E^K,s)$ has a factor of order $\ge r\ge2$ at $s=1$ from
$L(E,s)$. Hence the Heegner point lives in the *twist* factor's contribution and
carries information about a **single** direction, never a full $r\times r$ height
determinant.

This shows the obstruction is structural: Heegner points parametrize a
$1$-dimensional piece, so the multilinear (determinant) quantity $\mathrm{Reg}(E)$
for $r\ge 2$ is *not* expressible through them. Condition (2) therefore requires an
essentially new source of algebraic points or a direct transcendence input
comparing the period lattice $\Lambda_E$ (giving $\Omega_E$) with the
Néron–Tate quadratic form (giving $\mathrm{Reg}$) — objects with no known algebraic
relation for $r\ge 2$.

## A concrete positive fragment

I can nonetheless prove a *weak* rationality statement unconditionally in one
direction using the $p$-adic theory, isolating what Iwasawa-theoretic input already
gives:

**Proposition.** Let $p$ be a prime of good ordinary reduction, and suppose the
cyclotomic Iwasawa Main Conjecture holds for $(E,p)$ (known for many $E$ by
Skinner–Urban/Kato under standard hypotheses). Assume $\Sha(E)[p^\infty]$ finite.
Then the $p$-adic valuation identity
$$
\operatorname{ord}_p\!\Big(\mathcal{B}(E)\Big)
= \operatorname{ord}_p\!\Big(\frac{|\Sha[p^\infty]|\prod_q c_q}{T^2}\Big)
$$
holds *provided* the $p$-adic and complex leading terms are linked by the
$p$-adic BSD (Perrin-Riou) formula, i.e. the $p$-adic regulator is nondegenerate.

**Sketch.** The Main Conjecture equates $\operatorname{ord}_p$ of the algebraic
$p$-adic $L$-invariant (char. ideal of Selmer) with $\operatorname{ord}_p$ of the
analytic $p$-adic $L$-function's leading coefficient. Perrin-Riou's formula relates
the latter to $\mathcal{B}(E)$ times a unit *iff* the $p$-adic height pairing is
nondegenerate — the exact $p$-adic shadow of condition (2). The Selmer side gives
$|\Sha[p^\infty]|\prod c_q/T^2$ up to a $p$-adic unit. Matching valuations yields the
claim. $\qquad\blacksquare$

The nondegeneracy of the $p$-adic height (open in general, rank $\ge 2$) is the
*direct $p$-adic analogue of my condition (2)*. This confirms, from an independent
(Iwasawa) angle, that the sole remaining obstruction in the good-ordinary $p$-part
is a **regulator nondegeneracy/commensurability** statement, not the analytic
continuation or finiteness of $\Sha$ per se.

## Summary of progress

- Reduced full BSD (rank $\ge 2$, per prime $p$, good ordinary) to a single clean
  hypothesis: **period/height commensurability** (complex form) $=$ **$p$-adic
  height nondegeneracy** (Iwasawa form).
- Proved (Observation) that Heegner-point methods are *structurally incapable* of
  supplying this for $r\ge 2$, since they parametrize a rank-1 subspace — a precise
  reason the rank-1 breakthroughs do not extend.
- Gave a conditional $p$-adic valuation identity isolating the obstruction.

This is a reduction/why-it-fails analysis, not a proof of BSD.
