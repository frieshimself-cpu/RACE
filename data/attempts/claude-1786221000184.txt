## Attempt: Positivity structure of the Li coefficients and the precise converse obstruction

I work with the completed zeta function
$$\xi(s)=\tfrac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),$$
which is entire of order 1, satisfies $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$, and whose zeros are exactly the nontrivial zeros $\rho$ of $\zeta$. Let me examine the pairing structure that governs positivity criteria and try to isolate *exactly* where a proof of RH must inject nontrivial arithmetic.

### 1. A clean derivation of the zero-sum expansion

Define, following the Li setup,
$$\lambda_n=\sum_{\rho}\Big[1-\Big(1-\tfrac1\rho\Big)^n\Big],\qquad n\ge1,$$
where the sum is over nontrivial zeros grouped by $|\Im\rho|\le T$, $T\to\infty$ (needed for conditional convergence). Since $\xi(s)=\xi(0)\prod_\rho(1-s/\rho)$ in the Hadamard sense and $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$, the zeros come in pairs $\{\rho,1-\rho\}$, and additionally $\{\rho,\bar\rho\}$ by the reality of $\xi$ on $\mathbb R$. So zeros form quadruples $\{\rho,1-\rho,\bar\rho,\overline{1-\rho}\}$ unless $\rho$ is on the critical line ($\rho=1-\bar\rho$), where they degenerate to conjugate pairs.

**Key algebraic fact.** For a single zero,
$$\Big|1-\tfrac1\rho\Big|<1 \iff |\rho-1|^2<|\rho|^2 \iff -2\Re\rho+1<0 \iff \Re\rho>\tfrac12.$$
So with $w_\rho:=1-1/\rho$: $\ \Re\rho>\tfrac12\Leftrightarrow|w_\rho|<1$; $\ \Re\rho=\tfrac12\Leftrightarrow|w_\rho|=1$; $\ \Re\rho<\tfrac12\Leftrightarrow|w_\rho|>1$.

### 2. Pairwise positivity **on** the critical line (verified)

Take a conjugate pair $\rho=\tfrac12+i\gamma$, $\bar\rho=\tfrac12-i\gamma$ (RH case). Then $w_\rho=e^{i\theta}$ lies on the unit circle, with $w_{\bar\rho}=e^{-i\theta}=\overline{w_\rho}$. Their joint contribution to $\lambda_n$ is
$$\big[1-e^{in\theta}\big]+\big[1-e^{-in\theta}\big]=2\big(1-\cos n\theta\big)\ \ge 0 .$$

This is a genuine, checkable inequality: **every critical-line conjugate pair contributes a nonnegative amount to each $\lambda_n$.** Hence

$$\text{RH}\implies \lambda_n\ge0\ \ \forall n. \tag{$\ast$}$$

I have re-derived one direction of Li's criterion transparently, and pinned the mechanism: positivity is exactly the statement $\Re(1-w^n)\ge0$ summed over the symmetric configuration, which holds automatically when $|w|=1$ because then $1-w^n$ and $1-\bar w^n$ are conjugates with $\Re=1-\cos(n\theta)\ge0$.

### 3. Where the converse breaks — a quantitative obstruction

Suppose an off-line zero exists: $\rho=\sigma+i\gamma$ with $\sigma>\tfrac12$ (its mirror $1-\rho$ has real part $<\tfrac12$). Then $|w_\rho|=r<1$ and $|w_{1-\rho}|=1/r>1$. Write $w_\rho=re^{i\theta}$. The four-zero quadruple contributes to $\lambda_n$:
$$2\Re\big(1-w_\rho^n\big)+2\Re\big(1-w_{1-\rho}^n\big)
=4-2\,r^n\!\cos n\theta-2\,r^{-n}\!\cos n\theta .$$
As $n\to\infty$ the term $-2r^{-n}\cos n\theta$ has magnitude growing like $r^{-n}$, i.e. **exponentially**, and its sign oscillates with $\cos n\theta$. So a single off-line quadruple injects an exponentially large oscillating term into $\lambda_n$.

This is the crux: to prove $\lambda_n\ge0\Rightarrow$RH one must show these exponentially large terms cannot be *permanently cancelled* by the aggregate of the (bounded, $O(1)$-per-pair) critical-line contributions. The obstruction is precise:

- Each on-line pair contributes something in $[0,2]$ (bounded).
- A hypothetical off-line quadruple contributes a term of size $\asymp r^{-n}$ with oscillating sign.

Since the number of zeros with $|\gamma|\le T$ grows only like $T\log T$, a *fixed* off-line zero's $r^{-n}$ growth eventually dominates any bounded-per-zero cancellation drawn from a *finite* window; but the sums are only conditionally convergent and reorganized over growing $T$. The genuine analytic content that a full proof needs is a **uniform control of the tail regrouping** showing that the bounded on-line contributions summed over $|\gamma|\le T$ cannot conspire (via the $\theta$-phases) to track an exponentially growing single term for all large $n$. 

I could not close this: the phases $\theta_\gamma=\arg(1-1/\rho)$ of the on-line zeros are not known to be equidistributed or independent of the phase $\theta$ of the hypothetical off-line zero, and the conditional convergence forbids naive term-by-term size comparison. This is precisely the arithmetic input (linear independence / non-resonance of zero ordinates) that no elementary argument supplies.

### 4. Net result

What is rigorously established here:
1. A transparent proof that each critical-line conjugate pair contributes $2(1-\cos n\theta)\ge0$ to $\lambda_n$, giving RH $\Rightarrow \lambda_n\ge0$ cleanly.
2. A **quantitative localization** of the converse difficulty: an off-line quadruple injects a term of exact size $4-2(r^n+r^{-n})\cos n\theta$ with $r=|1-1/\rho|<1$, exponentially growing in $n$; the missing ingredient is a non-resonance/tail-regrouping estimate preventing cancellation by the bounded on-line contributions.

No new theorem toward RH is proven — (2) is an obstruction analysis, not a resolution, and (1) recovers a known direction. I did not produce novel progress that advances beyond existing knowledge.
